# Knowledge & Reverie

**Knowledge & Reverie** is {{Character:zeis}}'s core growth system. When Zeis touches a valid collectible pedestal on a normal floor, she does not take it like other characters. The item is sent into Death Certificate and kept as long-term **knowledge**.

Each piece of knowledge offers two paths:

> Permanently claim the item recorded as knowledge, or keep that knowledge and temporarily realize a nearby reverie

The relationship is always **knowledge → reverie**. Knowledge is the original item, certain but not yet cashed in. A reverie is a nearby item effect made real for this floor only, powered by that knowledge. A reverie never produces a second layer of reveries.

## Entering Death Certificate

From the first floor of the run, Zeis enters the Death Certificate area directly. On later floors she can re-enter from the starting-room entrance to manage existing knowledge and reveries.

Here Death Certificate is not the vanilla one-shot “enter once, take one item” room. It is a character space Zeis keeps returning to. The layout changes every floor, so which nearby items can become reveries for the same knowledge may also change.

## Newly encountered items

When Zeis touches a valid pedestal on a normal floor, the system first resolves its normal price and room costs, then removes the original pedestal and registers the item in Death Certificate. Shop purchases and similar costs are not bypassed by entering the knowledge system.

That registration is knowledge—it does **not** mean she already owns the item. Available knowledge maps to rooms in the area; rooms that contain those choices are automatically revealed on the minimap.

## Knowledge

Original items moved into Death Certificate become knowledge nodes that can be claimed permanently. Choosing the node itself grants Zeis that collectible for good, consumes the knowledge, and ends its future reverie options.

Each available knowledge also lights up valid collectibles within about **150** distance. Different visual states distinguish knowledge, available reveries, and pedestals that cannot be chosen right now.

## Reverie

A valid passive collectible within about 150 distance of active knowledge can become a **reverie**. Choosing it temporarily occupies that knowledge for the current floor and grants the target item’s temporary effect.

A reverie does not become a new knowledge center. The same knowledge supports only one decision per floor; after the next floor begins, the occupation ends, the original knowledge returns, and you may claim it permanently, pick the same reverie again, or choose a different nearby reverie.

Choosing a reverie is not taking a “nerfed item”—it keeps the right to decide that knowledge later.

## First-floor free choice

Floor 1 has a special rule: until that special choice is finished, Zeis may pick one valid passive as a temporary reverie without it needing to be lit by knowledge first.

That lets her get a temporary ability suited to the floor before any knowledge has built up. {{Collectible619}} expands this special choice and entrance rules, but does not turn temporary effects into permanent items.

## Temporary effects are not full pickups

A reverie means temporarily owning an item’s effect, not freely running a full vanilla pickup event. Items that permanently create resources or change character state on pickup get special handling, for example:

- heart containers do not stay permanently from temporary reveries;
- some life gains become limited healing;
- temporarily gained coins, keys, and bombs are clawed back on the next floor;
- some resource-spawning items produce only a limited one-shot result;
- temporary stat boosts clear when the floor ends.

You cannot borrow the same item every floor to stack pickup gains that should be permanent.

## End of floor

On the next floor, simulated items from reveries, temporary stats, and controlled resources are settled and cleared. Knowledge not permanently claimed stays in Death Certificate; knowledge used for a reverie last floor becomes choosable again; permanently claimed knowledge does not regenerate.

## Actives and charge

Actives can become knowledge too. When Zeis swaps to another active, the previous one keeps its charge and returns to its pedestal in Death Certificate. The active slot is not a one-time trade-off: the old active can still be claimed permanently, or used as knowledge so a nearby reverie becomes real this floor.

## Visual cues

In Death Certificate, think of pedestals in three states:

**Knowledge**

- an original item not yet claimed permanently;
- can be claimed for good;
- can light nearby reveries.

**Reverie**

- within range of valid knowledge;
- can be taken for this floor’s temporary effect.

**Unlinked items**

- no valid knowledge supporting them;
- cannot be taken as a normal choice this time.

## How to choose

Knowledge and reverie are not classic short-term vs long-term investment—they are certainty vs option value.

Claiming knowledge gives a certain permanent item and closes future reveries around it. Choosing a reverie temporarily forgoes that permanent gain but keeps the decision for later floors. Quality alone is not the only axis; where knowledge sits in the Death Certificate layout also decides which future choices it can open.
