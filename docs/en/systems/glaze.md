# Glaze

Glaze is a family of special content that shares visuals and themes, including glazed pickups, chests, bombs, enemies, and items that interact with them.

Different Glaze pieces have their own effects. Holding several Glaze items does not automatically form a set bonus. See each entry page for the exact rules.

## Glazed pickups

Public glazed pickups currently include special hearts, coins, bombs, batteries, grab bags, and chests.

They can appear through Glaze items, cards, and conversion effects.

## Glaze Chest

{{Pickup:glaze-chest}} places a matching key in another room on this floor; find that key to open it.
