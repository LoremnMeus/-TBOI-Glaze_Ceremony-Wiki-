## Effects

**On first visit to a room, Torn Emperor has about a 25% chance to open one extra door at random.**

The doors follow the same opening rules as the Thoth card {{Card:iv-the-emperor}}, so they can connect to other rooms reachable on the current floor.

Each effective copy rolls separately; more successes can open more doors in that room entry.

## Trivia

- Torn Emperor comes from this mod's Thoth card {{Card:iv-the-emperor}}.
- It is not only a name-and-sprite fragment—the door-opening effect reuses the Emperor card's logic directly.
