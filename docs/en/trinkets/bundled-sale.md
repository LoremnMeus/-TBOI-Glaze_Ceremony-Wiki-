## Effects

**Bundled Sale doubles shop prices, but each successful purchase randomly gifts another still-priced good.**

- priced goods cost ×2;
- after a completed purchase, one random remaining priced good becomes free (price 0);
- if no other priced goods remain, nothing extra is gifted.

## Tips

- With several goods in the shop, the first purchase can grant a free leftover item; buying the last remaining priced good leaves no gift target.
