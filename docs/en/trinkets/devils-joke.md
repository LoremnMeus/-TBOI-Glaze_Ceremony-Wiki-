## Effects

**Entering a Devil Room, Devil's Joke hands over a temporary {{Collectible:105}} while quietly scrambling the room's entry door direction.**

On the first visit to a Devil Room:

- the holder receives {{Collectible:105}} in an extra pocket active slot;
- the Devil Room's enter / leave direction is rewritten.

So if the move key toward the door stays held while entering, the character may keep walking after the room loads and leave immediately.

## Tips

- **Release the movement keys after entering the Devil Room.** Stop inside first, then use the gifted {{Collectible:105}}. Avoiding the doorway shove makes this roughly a free D6 per Devil Room.
