## Effects

**When Straining Symbol appears as a ground trinket, it instantly clears hostile projectiles in the room.**

Dropping the ordinary form triggers it; other spawns that place the ordinary trinket on the floor follow the same rule. A golden copy on the ground currently does not clear projectiles.

## Tips

- In a dangerous bullet pattern, drop the trinket to clear the screen; pick it back up to use the clear again later.

## Trivia

- Straining Symbol corresponds to **Autio**, a character not yet fully implemented.
- It belongs to the six character Symbol trinkets.
