## Effects

**Hoarding Symbol empties current coins, keys, and bombs in exchange for a damage up that survives losing the trinket.**

On a valid pickup:

- coins drop to 0;
- keys drop to 0;
- bombs drop to 0;
- {{Damage}} permanently +1 Damage.

Losing the trinket afterward does not remove that damage gain.

## Tips

- **Spend coins, keys, and bombs you planned to use before picking it up.** Only the inventory still held at the moment of pickup is wiped, so shopping, unlocking, or bombing first does not reduce the permanent damage reward.

## Trivia

- Hoarding Symbol corresponds to {{Character:anna}}. Anna's kit centers on gathering and storing matter, so the trinket spends already-hoarded resources in one dump.
- It belongs to the six character Symbol trinkets.
