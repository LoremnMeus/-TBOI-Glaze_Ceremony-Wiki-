## Effects

**Pacification Mark pulls every priced shop item in the room onto the same average.**

While held, charged goods in the current room take the arithmetic mean of their prices, round that mean up, and all settle at that common price.

For example, items priced **5 / 10 / 15** average to 10, so all three end up costing 10.
