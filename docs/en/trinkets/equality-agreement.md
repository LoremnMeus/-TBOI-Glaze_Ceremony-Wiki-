## Effects

**When the shop visit ends with zero coins, Equality Agreement turns remaining priced resource goods into item pedestals.**

While the holder has **0** coins in a shop:

- priced hearts, keys, bombs, batteries, and other non-item goods become random collectibles;
- goods that are already items are not rewritten again;
- spending the last coin inside the shop also triggers the conversion immediately.

## Tips

- **You do not need to enter the shop already at 0 coins.** Buy what you want first, then spend the last coin; remaining priced resource goods convert as soon as coins hit 0.
