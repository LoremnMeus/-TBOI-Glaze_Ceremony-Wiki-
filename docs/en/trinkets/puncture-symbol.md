## Effects

**Puncture Symbol hurts on pickup in exchange for tears that pierce both enemies and obstacles while held.**

- Each ordinary copy deals **2** pickup damage in total;
- while held, tears gain piercing and spectral.

## Trivia

- Puncture Symbol is one of the character symbols for {{Character:tecro}}; its piercing theme echoes Tecro's spear-through combat.
- It belongs to the six character Symbol trinkets.
