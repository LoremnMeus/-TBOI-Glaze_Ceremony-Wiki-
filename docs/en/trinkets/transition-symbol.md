## Effects

**Walking through a door into an unvisited room has a 2% chance to be redirected into an Error Room.**

Already-visited rooms do not roll this check.

More effective copies raise the chance.

## Trivia

- Transition Symbol corresponds to {{Character:zeis}}, extending Zeis's theme of unusual rooms and spatial travel by occasionally diverting the normal path.
- It belongs to the six character Symbol trinkets.
