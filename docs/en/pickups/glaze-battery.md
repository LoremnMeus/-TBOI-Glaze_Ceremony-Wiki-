## Mechanics

## Effects

A Glaze Battery stores one charge-refund: the next time a normal active item **actually spends charges**, half of the charges spent are refunded immediately (rounded up).

Example: spending 6 refunds 3; spending 1 still refunds 1.

A Car Battery second activation does not consume this effect. The pickup can only be collected if the player already has at least one active item.
