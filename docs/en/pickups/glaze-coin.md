## Mechanics

## Effects

A Glaze Coin slowly attracts nearby pickups; collecting it then rolls a coin payout.

| Result | Chance |
| --- | ---: |
| 1¢ | 90% |
| 5¢ | 10% |

Attraction only pushes nearby non-shop pickups; it does not auto-collect them for the player. Shop items are ignored.

With {{Item:crown-of-the-glaze}} at full shards, the high payout becomes **15¢** (still 10%).
