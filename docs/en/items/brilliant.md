## Effects

- Gain **3 Golden Hearts**.
- Each Golden Heart reduces coin prices in shops by 1.
- When Brilliant itself appears in a shop, its price cannot exceed your current coin count; if you have no coins, it still costs at least **1 coin**.

Once Brilliant is owned, more Golden Hearts mean larger discounts on other coin-priced items, down to free.

<details>
<summary>Technical Details</summary>

- In co-op, the discount uses the total Golden Hearts held by all players.

</details>
