## Effects

**Devil's Heart binds a two-way death pact with one enemy: whichever side would die first makes the other pay.**

Use it to lift, then press a fire key to throw a seed. Hitting a vulnerable enemy makes them the host.

| Who would die first | Result |
| --- | --- |
| The player takes lethal damage | Player death is cancelled; warp to the host, who pays. Normal enemies die; bosses lose about **12%** max HP and cannot be finished by that hit |
| A non-boss host takes lethal damage | Host death is cancelled and HP is set to about **20%** max; the player takes **1** full heart of damage |
| A boss host takes lethal damage | The pact clears; the player does not pay |

Each time a host successfully dies for the player, a costume layer is added (up to four) to mark how often the pact has paid out.

## Special interactions

### {{Collectible:584}}

Consuming a wisp: a normal host is heavily wounded instead of killed; boss pay damage is halved.

### {{Collectible:34}}

After a host dies for the player, gain a temporary room damage up.

### {{Seija}}

When the player pays for the host, damage is only half a heart; when the host pays for the player, the player still takes a small hit.

### {{Collectible:706}}

The matching Abyss locust can also plant the pact on hit.
