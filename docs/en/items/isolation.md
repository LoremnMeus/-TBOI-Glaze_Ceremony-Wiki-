## Mechanics

## Effects

At the start of each run, the game draws 10 distinct unlocked collectibles into a fixed clique. Those members stay in their normal item pools and can appear as usual. Not holding Isolation does not change how other items generate.

While you hold Isolation, encountering any clique member on a normal pedestal expands that offer into a full 10-choose-1. The original member remains one of the options, and the other nine appear as real collectible pedestals in the same mutually exclusive option group. You may take only one; the rest disappear.

Isolation's cost is paid only after a successful pickup from that party: one copy of Isolation is removed and dropped nearby as a rejected pedestal that cannot be picked up again. Rejection persists across room exits, re-entry, and Save & Continue.

## Clique Selection

- Candidates are unlocked, non-hidden, non-quest/story collectibles from the base game and mods, including actives, passives, and familiars.
- Isolation itself is never a clique member.
- Per-candidate quality weights are 1.0 for qualities 0–3 and 0.15 for quality 4.
- A clique may include at most one quality-4 item.
- Once drawn, the list never changes mid-run. Members already obtained normally may still appear as options later.

## Notes

- Shops, existing option groups, Death Certificate displays, and other unsafe scenes skip the party and leave the original pedestal alone.
- The first encountered member is the host; the other nine are companions. If the host is externally rerolled or replaced, the party dissolves, companions are removed, and Isolation is not spent.
- If a companion is externally rerolled or replaced, that companion is removed and does not leave a new keepable reward; the host and remaining options stay.
- Normal option settlement is not treated as an external morph.
- The clique is shared for the whole run. With multiple copies of Isolation, each successful party spends one copy; any remaining copy can trigger again.
- If a rejected Isolation pedestal becomes a different item, the rejection behavior no longer applies.
