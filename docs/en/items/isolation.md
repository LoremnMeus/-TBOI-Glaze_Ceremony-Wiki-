## Mechanics

## Effects

**Each run, 10 random items form a clique that keeps Isolation out.**

While holding Isolation, encountering any clique member offers all **10** items to choose from.

Choosing one joins the clique and removes one Isolation. The excluded Isolation drops on the ground and refuses to be picked up again.

## Mechanics details

- Clique members stay fixed for the run, and at most **1** quality-4 item is included.
- Multiple Isolation copies can each trigger; each choice spends only one copy.
- An excluded Isolation still cannot be picked up after leaving and returning to the room.

- Isolation is a {{System:rainbow}} item in this mod, and its icon is original art.
