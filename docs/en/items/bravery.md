## Mechanics

## Effects

Bravery abandons players who flee from an unfinished battle and refuses to be picked up again.

- {{Damage}} Each copy grants +2 Damage.
- Fleeing an uncleared {{BossRoom}} Boss Room, {{ChallengeRoom}} Challenge Room, or {{BossRushRoom}} Boss Rush causes Bravery to reject the player.
- Bravery drops upon arrival in the next room, removing one copy and its Damage bonus from the player.

Bravery considers the following actions fleeing:

- Leaving immediately after using an item, card, or pill, including teleports that occur after an extra animation.
- Walking out through a door.
- Leaving through a portal.
- Descending to the next floor before finishing the battle.

Bravery only judges a departure once the player actually leaves. Merely using an item or approaching an exit does not trigger rejection.

## Notes

- Boss Challenge Rooms are also included.
- Leaving a cleared room or an ordinary room does not trigger rejection.
- Rejected Bravery cannot be picked up by any player. On contact, it says "Coward." or "I refuse you."
- If the rejected Bravery is replaced by another item, it says "Kill me, then."
- Rejection applies only to that particular copy. Other copies of Bravery found later can still be picked up normally.
