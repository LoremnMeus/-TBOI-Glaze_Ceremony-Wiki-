## Effects

**Keep firing to charge Skiel. At full charge, it unfolds a factor web of tears linked together.**

Among the three Factors, Skiel has the shortest charge time and is the easiest to trigger repeatedly.

## How to Use

- Hold fire to charge.
- Charge progress appears on the dedicated bar beside the character.
- At full charge, the factor web fires.
- Releasing fire early clears the current charge and you must start over.

## Factor Web

On trigger, several waves of tears spread outward.

These tears:

- fan out from the current firing direction
- link to neighboring tears
- deal about half of your damage each
- spawn in short succession, forming an expanding web.

Firing also nudges you slightly along the attack direction.

## The Three Factors

The pedestal cycles Skiel → Wisel → Granel; **you receive whichever form is shown when picked up.** It stops changing afterward.

## Synergies

For each other Factor you hold, this item's charge rate increases by 15% (115% with two, 130% with all three).
