## Effects

Gold Rush can turn some rocks in newly entered rooms into fool's gold. Destroying fool's gold may reveal veins of different value, a special find, or simply a nest of startled rats.

Each piece of fool's gold rolls its result independently:

| Outcome | Chance | Contents |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Rat nest | 35% | 2–4 rats flee from the rock; they do not attack, and killing them grants no reward |
| Small vein | 45% | 1–4 {{Coin}} |
| Rich vein | 15% | 5–10 {{Coin}} of total value, sometimes including a nickel |
| Special find | 5% | A lucky penny, golden bomb, golden spiders, or Troll Bomb |

The more fool's gold has already appeared during the current streak, the less likely additional rocks are to transform. This decay resets after 3–5 newly entered rooms.

Individual cells that belong to a large rock can also become fool's gold. Only the selected cell transforms; the remaining parts adjust to the rock structure that is left instead of causing the whole formation to transform.

Additional copies of Gold Rush increase the base chance for rocks to become fool's gold. They also increase the number of golden spiders produced by that special result.

## Synergies

### {{Collectible:706}}

When absorbed by Abyss, Gold Rush creates a golden locust with a 5% chance to turn enemies gold on hit.

## Trivia

A gold rush is a period of intense pursuit of newly discovered gold, while fool's gold refers to minerals that resemble gold without sharing its value.

The tagline “All that glitters...” reflects the uncertain contents of fool's gold: a gleaming rock may contain treasure, or merely a nest of startled rats.
