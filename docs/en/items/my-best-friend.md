## Effects

Qing treats the odd heads that sometimes tumble out of chests as newly met “friends.”

Picking this up immediately spawns **2 random chests**.

Afterwards:

- when a Golden Chest would grant a collectible, that collectible is chosen from the “friend” list instead;
- Normal and Spiked Chests have a **10%** chance to replace their drop with a “friend.”

Finding a friend gives a clear cue.

While My Best Friend is held, those items keep Qing's “friend” nicknames in EID even if they appear from another source such as a Treasure Room.

## Notes

- Golden Chests only swap to a friend when they would already produce a collectible; they do not invent an extra item from nothing.
- Holding extra copies does not raise the 10% friend chance on Normal and Spiked Chests.
