## Mechanics

<!-- Manual body. The exporter never overwrites this file once it exists. -->

Qing considers a hand-picked group of head-themed or companion-like items to be her “friends.” Picking this item up immediately spawns **2 random chests**. Afterwards, collectible rewards from golden chests use the friend list, while normal and spiked chests have about a **10%** chance to produce a friend instead.

The list currently includes Cricket's Head, Tammy's Head, Bob's Rotten Head, Steven, Guppy's Head, Abel, Goat Head, Fate's Reward, Head of the Keeper, Voodoo Head, and Decap Attack. Finding one produces a colored poof and happy cue.

Golden chests are only changed when they would already create a collectible. Extra copies do not increase the replacement chance.
