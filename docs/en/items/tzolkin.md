## Effects

Give Tzolkin one held item in exchange for an equally temporary 3-choice. When hit, Tzolkin blocks the damage and takes those temporary items away until it is found again.

On use:

1. Choose one held passive / familiar item;
2. That item becomes temporary;
3. Spawn a 3-choice set based on its quality;
4. Pick one;
5. The new item is also part of this temporary set.

## Quality

Candidates prefer the **same quality** as the chosen item. If fewer than 3 valid options exist, nearby qualities are included—so the set is not guaranteed to be all one quality.

## Loss and restore

Taking damage loses Tzolkin and blocks that hit; linked temporary items leave with it. Obtaining Tzolkin again restores those temporary items.

## Notes

- Temporary pedestals use a distinct look.
- After loss, the next collectible drawn from the item pool is forced to be Tzolkin.
