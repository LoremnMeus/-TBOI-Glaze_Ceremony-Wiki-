## Effects

**Using World Arc grants a temporary random passive, then hides the item in another room on the floor. Find it again to reuse it and keep stacking temporary passives.**

On each use:

- Gain **1 floor-long random passive**;
- World Arc leaves the active slot;
- It reappears in another random room on the floor;
- Pick it up to use it again.

Temporary passives from repeated uses on the same floor **stack and stay active**; new rolls do not replace old ones.

Entering the next floor clears all of those temporary passives.

### Finding World Arc again

It can hide in most other rooms on the floor, including **rooms already explored**.

It never hides in the current room, and never in Ultra Secret Rooms.

So after clearing many rooms and then using it, you may still need to backtrack.

If it is never reclaimed this floor, it is not lost forever; on the next floor it is reassigned to a random room.
