## Effects

**Each use flaps Isaac upward once. Mash it like Flappy Bird to climb again before landing.**

- One use: rise, then fall.
- Use again while falling: lift again.
- Keep using: raise and hold height.

High enough, Isaac can pass over obstacles and enemies.

Stop using, and he drifts back down to normal collision.

This is not permanent Flight; clearance only lasts while you keep flapping.

## Synergies

## Trivia

A conceptual flip of vanilla {{Collectible:282}} **How to Jump**.

How to Jump takes Mario-style hops; How to Fly takes **Flappy Bird**—repeated active uses to flap and hold altitude.

The flavor line “Tap Tap tap!” points at that cadence.
