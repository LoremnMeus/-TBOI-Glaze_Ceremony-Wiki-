## Effects

**Cloundy is a familiar that “optimizes” the room on its own: it digests and converts targets, and offers “game advice” that is not always correct.**

It looks for enemies, basic pickups, and some interactables, then decides whether to interfere:

- Digesting a normal enemy turns it into a basic pickup
- Digesting a basic pickup may turn it back into an enemy
- It sometimes throws bombs at doors, machines, or other targets it considers “worth handling”
- While acting, it also delivers its own “game advice”

It is not a pure benefit familiar. It keeps reshaping room resources and enemies—sometimes creating extras, sometimes removing something useful.

## Digestion

Cloundy searches the room for digestible targets.

Normally it can handle:

- Normal enemies
- Basic pickups such as {{Coin}}, {{Key}}, {{Bomb}}, and {{Heart}}

Item pedestals are not treated as basic pickups by a normal Cloundy.

Some important bosses and special enemies are excluded, so it does not break the run progression outright.

## “Game advice”

Besides digestion, Cloundy also interferes with the room on its own from time to time.

It may:

- Throw bombs toward directions it thinks hide a secret room
- Try to bomb certain machines
- Try to handle certain beggars or other interactables
- Comment on what is happening

None of this is guaranteed to be correct or helpful.

In short, Cloundy is not stable support—it is a self-appointed “coach” that makes its own calls.

## Multiple copies

Each copy of Cloundy spawns an additional familiar.

Multiple Cloundies act separately, so:

- Digestion and conversion happen more often
- Bomb throws and other interference also increase
- The room becomes much less predictable

Challenge {{Challenge:pointing-and-disappointing}} is built around multiple Cloundies.

## Synergies

### {{Seija}}

When boosted, Cloundy’s enemy ↔ basic-pickup swap expands further:

- Bosses can be turned into items
- Items can be turned into bosses

That greatly increases how much it can rewrite the room.

## Notes

- Cloundy acts automatically; there is no direct control over its next target.
- It may consume valuable basic pickups, so it is unreliable when specific resources must be kept.
- Normally, item pedestals are outside its basic-pickup digestion targets.
- Some important bosses are excluded from normal digestion.
- Multiple Cloundies act independently and do not merge into one stronger familiar.

## Trivia

Cloundy’s joke comes from the online “cloud player” stereotype: someone who is not really playing, yet confidently delivers guides, judgments, and advice.

Its lines continue that joke—the advice usually sounds certain, but it is not necessarily right.
