## Effects

Wishing can turn into a collectible of your choice, but must first be placed on a pedestal and rerolled.

Using it presents 3 random collectibles to choose as a wish. Rejecting them all adds 1 extra option on the next use. Using Wishing again allows the wish to be changed.

Choosing or rejecting costs 1 charge. Rerolling a wished Wishing pedestal turns it into the chosen collectible.

## Synergies

### {{Collectible:584}}

Each wish spawns a fragile wisp. When the wish comes true, all surviving Wishing wisps become wisps of the wished collectible.

### {{Collectible:34}}

Each selection includes at least one Devil Room collectible, regardless of how many items remain in the pool.
