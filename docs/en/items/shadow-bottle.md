## Effects

On **first** entry to an uncleared room, each Shadow Bottle summons 1 random friendly shadow enemy.

Shadows come from the mod's Shadow / Shaddoll enemy pool and fight as temporary allies in that room.

## Notes

- Only triggers on first visit while the room is still uncleared.
- Extra copies stack additional summons.
