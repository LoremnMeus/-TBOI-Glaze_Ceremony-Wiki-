## Effects

Giant Punch reverses between a buff and a penalty according to current health relative to the character's normal health capacity.

| Current health | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| Below normal capacity | Size ×1.25 and {{Damage}} damage ×2 |
| Equal to normal capacity | No change |
| Above normal capacity | Size ×0.75 and {{Damage}} damage ×0.5 |

For ordinary characters, normal capacity consists of red-heart containers and bone-heart capacity. Soul, black, and eternal hearts increase current health without increasing that capacity. For example, with 6 red-heart containers, 5 hearts triggers growth, healing to 6 returns to normal, and adding a soul heart triggers shrinking.

## Special characters

A character with no red-heart capacity but another health resource uses their current health when Giant Punch is first obtained as a fixed baseline. Falling below that baseline causes growth; exceeding it causes shrinking. Characters with no comparable health resource remain neutral.

## Tips

Healing and collecting soul hearts are no longer unconditional combat upgrades. Maintaining double damage may require leaving a health gap or refusing extra health that would push the total above normal capacity.

## Trivia

The effect references the Yu-Gi-Oh! Equip Spell “Megamorph.” The original card doubles or halves a monster's original ATK according to the players' relative Life Points; this mod translates that reversal into a comparison between current health and normal health capacity.
