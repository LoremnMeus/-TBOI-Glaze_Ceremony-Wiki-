## Effects

Blaststone lets you double-tap a fire direction to launch an extra, slowly detonating brimstone fireball.

After launch, the fireball pulls nearby enemies toward itself. After a short buildup, it bursts brimstone outward in several waves.

The fireball inherits some of your current brimstone look and attack presentation, so items that change brimstone can also change how Blaststone looks.

## Notes

* Blaststone has its own cooldown; spamming double-taps will not keep spawning fireballs.
* Pull is weaker the farther an enemy is; enemies too far away are not attracted.
* Once bursting starts, the fireball fires multiple ring attacks rather than a single blast.

## Tips

Aim the fireball into a group of enemies. Pull them together first, then let the following brimstone rings hit several targets at once.
