## Effects

**Cheater's Blessing is a hidden console easter egg.**

In a run, after the **3rd completed `rewind`**, it appears near Isaac.

On pickup:

- Gain one Holy Card shield;
- Gain a very small all-stats up.

Other rewind forms such as Glowing Hour Glass do not count toward the `rewind` total.

## Synergies

### {{Seija}}

Taking damage immediately triggers one Glowing Hour Glass rewind.
