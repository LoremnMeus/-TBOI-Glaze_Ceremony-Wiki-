## Effects

**Cheater's Blessing is a console easter egg: after the third real `rewind` finishes in a run, it appears near Isaac.**

It is not normal item-pool content. On pickup:

- gain one Holy Mantle-style shield (as if using a Holy Card);
- tiny ups to speed, tears, damage, luck, and shot speed;
- a tiny range up as well.

## How it appears

Only game-completed `rewind`s count. The third successful rewind in the same run drops the item nearby.

It is not “press a key three times”—only actual rewinds that finish count.

## Why you rarely see it

It is a hidden easter egg and will not naturally appear in treasure rooms, shops, or devil deals.

## Synergies

### {{Seija}}

On hit, force-triggers Glowing Hourglass.

## Notes

The stat ups are tiny—more souvenir than build centerpiece.
