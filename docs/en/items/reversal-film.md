## Effects

Reversal Film is a key item for the shadow story route. Holding it provides no ordinary passive combat effect.

## Use

It appears on a specific story route and interacts with story doors / acts as that route's key item.

<details>
<summary>Story role (spoilers)</summary>

After the related shadow story begins on Depths II, clearing that floor's Boss Room may spawn Reversal Film. Picking it up there immediately returns the player to the floor's starting room.

While holding it near the “go home” door, it can be consumed to open a Death Certificate route. It can also open certain shadow doors (one option alongside {{Collectible:327}} / {{Collectible:328}}).

</details>

## Notes

- Holding it does not provide an ongoing combat effect.
- Spawn and door behavior depend on shadow story progress.
