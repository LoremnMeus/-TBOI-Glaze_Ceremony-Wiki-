## Effects

Reversal Film is a **story item**, not a normal build-around collectible. There is no standalone passive combat script; a shadow-route story thread consumes it.

## Use

It appears on a specific story route and interacts with story doors / acts as that route's key item.

<details>
<summary>Story role (spoilers)</summary>

After the Depths II shadow thread starts, clearing that floor's boss room may spawn Reversal Film. Picking it up in that boss room immediately teleports back to the floor's starting room (room index 84).

While holding it near the “go home” door, it can be consumed to open a Death Certificate route. It can also open certain shadow doors (one option alongside {{Collectible:327}} / {{Collectible:328}}).

</details>

## Notes

- Do not treat it as a combat passive with ongoing effects.
- Spawn and door behavior depend on shadow story progress.
