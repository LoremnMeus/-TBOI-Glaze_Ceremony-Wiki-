## Effects

On use it:

- shuffles the player's current stackable costumes and reapplies the **same count** at random;
- “rerolls” every collectible pedestal in the room **into itself** (same ID);
- renames the item from D773 to D774, D775… advancing one digit each use.

## Special interactions

### {{Collectible:34}}

Adds **3** extra devil-themed costumes on top.

### {{Collectible:584}}

Spawns a wisp; when it dies, a random room pickup is “rerolled” into itself.

## Trivia

The English flavor line *Life is like an Oreo* ties the die-number gag to “swap the wrapper, keep the same filling.”
