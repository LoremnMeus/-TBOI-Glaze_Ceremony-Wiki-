## Effects

Drama of sorrow and joy occasionally turns tears into alternating tragedy and comedy mask tears that mark foes.

About 15% of shots may convert. Tragedy and comedy strictly alternate.

## Tragedy

When a tragedy-marked foe dies, a delayed chasing death echo appears—an explosion that pursues nearby enemies. Base damage is about `2 × Damage` plus a small share of the target's max HP; boss HP bonus is capped by damage.

## Comedy

When a comedy-marked foe dies, a brief friendly actor spawns (~120 frames) and attacks nearby enemies.

## Tragicomedy

A foe marked with both masks enters Tragicomedy. On death it triggers an empowered tragedy echo and an empowered comedy actor (~180 frames).

## Notes

- Marks must be applied before death for the exit effect to fire.
- Both masks on one target are required for Tragicomedy.
