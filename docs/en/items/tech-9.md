## Effects

Attacks have a chance to fire an extra Technology laser or Tech X ring. More Luck and more copies make that happen more often. Supported attack styles beyond normal tears can trigger it too.

<details>
<summary>Technical details</summary>

- Each trigger appends at most one Technology attack; the item tries a Tech X ring and several Technology laser shapes in a fixed order and stops on the first success.
- Branches use different base rates and Luck weights; extra copies raise the matching chances.

</details>
