## Effects

Tech IX occasionally tacks one more Technology weapon onto the attack you already fired.

That extra shot may be a Technology laser or a Tech X ring. More Luck and more copies of Tech IX make the follow-up show up more often.

It is not tear-only—some other supported attack styles can trigger it too.

<details>
<summary>Technical details</summary>

- Each trigger appends at most one Technology attack; several shapes are tried in a fixed order and stop on the first success.
- Shapes use different base rates and Luck weights; extra copies raise the matching chances.

</details>

## Trivia

“IX” is the Roman numeral 9. The name nods to Apple’s jump from iPhone 8 straight to iPhone X—the missing iPhone 9. The Chinese subtitle “被跳过的未来” and the English flavor “Skipped, looped, returned.” both play on that skipped number.
