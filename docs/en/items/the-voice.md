## Effects

**The Voice no longer needs the book.**

It is released from {{Item:book-of-voice}} via the final offer. Whispers still begin on their own; when one needs an answer, a phantom briefly remains in the active slot—use it to accept or refuse. Confirming pays the demand and grants the promise immediately.

Compliance, deal types, and synergies match the book phase; see {{Item:book-of-voice}}.

## Notes

- The phantom answers the current whisper; it is not a separate permanent active build piece.
- Compliance and permanent damage gained before release are kept.
