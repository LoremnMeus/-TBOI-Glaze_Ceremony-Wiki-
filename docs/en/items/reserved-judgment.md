## Effects

**Reserved Judgment can carry one priced item's current offer to the next floor. You can reserve only one at a time; reserving a new item replaces the old reservation.**

While holding this item, stand near a priced collectible and press {{ButtonRT}} to reserve that trade.

Reserving does not buy or remove the item. It stays where it is and can still be bought normally on the current floor.

If that trade is still valid when you enter the next floor, the same item appears in the starting room at the reserved price.

## Reservation rules

Each player can reserve only **1** item at a time.

Reserving a new item immediately cancels the previous reservation.

The recorded price is whatever was shown when you pressed {{ButtonRT}}. Later price changes on the source item do not change the reserved quote.

If the source item is bought, or the original trade is truly replaced by another mechanism, the reservation cancels automatically.

## Special rule

When Reserved Judgment itself is sold as a priced item, you can press {{ButtonRT}} to reserve it even if you do not currently hold the item.

This exception applies only to Reserved Judgment itself.
