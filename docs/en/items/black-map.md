## Effects

Black Map reveals rooms on the floor early, then gradually erases the places you have already walked.

While held, every unvisited room on the floor appears on the map. After you enter a room, it tries to disappear from the minimap, so the map slowly shrinks to “places you have not gone yet.”

Other mapping effects can stop certain rooms from being erased.

## Synergies

### {{Collectible:21}}

Special rooms revealed by the Compass do not disappear from the map.

### {{Collectible:54}}

Normal rooms revealed by the Treasure Map do not disappear from the map.

### {{Collectible:246}}

Secret rooms revealed by the Blue Map do not disappear from the map.

### {{Collectible:333}}

With full-map effects, rooms you have already entered stay on the map as well.

## Tips

Black Map is best for finding routes you have not explored yet: once visited areas fade away, the rooms left on the map are basically your next targets.
