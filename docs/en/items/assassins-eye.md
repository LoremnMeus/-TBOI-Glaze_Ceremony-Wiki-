## Effects

Assassin's Eye lets flying tears look for openings and suddenly strike nearby enemies.

After an assassination, the tear dashes through the target and keeps flying. The same tear does not assassinate endlessly; it needs a short interval before seeking another target.

Some other attack styles can also trigger the effect, shown as extra assassin tears.
