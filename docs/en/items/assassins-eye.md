## Effects

Assassin's Eye makes tears seize openings to strike nearby enemies while in flight.

After finding a target, a tear quickly slashes through it and continues flying. The same tear has a short interval between assassinations, but can seek another target afterward. Some non-tear weapons are supported by spawning additional assassin tears.

When absorbed by Abyss, its locust also lunges at nearby enemies.
