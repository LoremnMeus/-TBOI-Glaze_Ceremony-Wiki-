## Effects

World's End Tianyi occasionally lets a traveler from another worldline appear during exploration. She wears Tianyi's likeness, crosses from one side of the room, and carries a small gift.

Travelers only appear in safe rooms with no combat. After appearing, she walks toward the other side on her own; approach her before she leaves and brush past her, and she leaves the reward she is carrying.

The reward is visible when she appears. Most of the time it is basic resources such as coins, bombs, keys, hearts, or cards, with a smaller chance of chests, runes, or other rarer drops.

Each copy of World's End Tianyi can provide at most one successful meeting per floor. Missing a traveler has no penalty.

## Notes

* You do not need to collide with her; getting close enough completes the meeting.
* After a successful meeting reward this floor, no new traveler appears on this floor unless you hold extra copies of World's End Tianyi.
* Travelers do not appear in active combat rooms, Boss Rooms, Devil Rooms, Angel Rooms, or other hard-scripted event rooms.
* If you miss her, she leaves with the reward.

## Tips

The reward she carries is visible when she appears. If you do not need what she is holding, there is no need to go out of your way to catch her.

## Trivia

World's End Tianyi draws from “world's end” and Tianyi figures across different worldlines.

The old version was a follower baby that charged and fired a Revelation-like beam. The remake removes the permanent familiar and turns “Tianyi from another world” into a worldline traveler who briefly appears during exploration, then may be missed again.
