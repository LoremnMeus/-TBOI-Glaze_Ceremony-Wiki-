## Effects

World's End Tianyi occasionally causes a traveler from another worldline to pass through the room after it is cleared. She carries a small gift as she crosses from one side of the room.

Approach her before she leaves. She will stop, hand over the gift she is carrying, then continue on her way. Missing the traveler has no penalty.

Additional copies increase the chance for a traveler to appear:

| Copies | Chance |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | 20% |
| 2 | 30% |
| 3 | 38% |
| 4 | 45% |
| 5+ | 50% |

### Gifts

The traveler displays the gift she is carrying when you meet her. Possible rewards include:

* Coins
* Hearts
* Bombs
* Keys
* Batteries
* Runes
* Chests
* Collectibles

Collectibles are comparatively rare and are drawn from the Treasure Room pool.

## Notes

* You do not need to collide with the traveler; getting close enough completes the meeting.
* Only one traveler can be active at a time. A new encounter will not begin while another traveler is still present.
* A traveler only appears in rooms she can walk through.
* {{ErrorRoom}} Error Rooms, Dungeon rooms, {{BossRushRoom}} Boss Rush, and Black Markets cannot trigger a traveler.
* {{BossRoom}} Boss Rooms, {{DevilRoom}} Devil Rooms, and {{AngelRoom}} Angel Rooms are not explicitly excluded by World's End Tianyi; whether an encounter can occur still depends on the room producing a normal room-clear event.
* If you do not approach her in time, she leaves with the gift.

## Trivia

World's End Tianyi draws from the idea of “world's end” and Tianyi figures from different worldlines.

The old version was a follower familiar that charged and fired a Revelation-like beam. The remake removes the permanent familiar and turns “Tianyi from another world” into a worldline traveler who briefly appears during exploration before departing again.
