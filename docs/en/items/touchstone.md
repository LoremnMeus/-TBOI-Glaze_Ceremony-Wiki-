## Effects

Touchstone lets other characters borrow Qing's blade style for one room.

On use, normal attacks are replaced by Qing's blade system. The blades still read the character's current damage, weapon type, tear flags, and multishot, so their behavior changes with the build. The original attack returns upon entering the next room. Repeated uses in the same room only maintain the style and do not add attacks.

## Special synergies

- While holding {{Collectible:34}}, the blade style additionally behaves as though Brimstone were owned.
- When absorbed by Abyss, its locust performs a stabbing attack when it hits an enemy.
