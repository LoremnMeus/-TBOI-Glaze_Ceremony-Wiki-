## Effects

Touchstone temporarily replaces your current build with Qing's blade attacks.

On use, normal attacks in the current room are replaced by Qing's blade style. The blades still read your current weapon type, shot count, and tear effects, so different builds do not borrow the same fixed attack pattern.

## Notes

- Your original attack returns when you enter another room.
- Using it again in the same room only maintains the blade style and does not add extra attacks.
