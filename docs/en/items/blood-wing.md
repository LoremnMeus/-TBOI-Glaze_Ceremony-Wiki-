## Effects

**Blood Wing charges while hugging a wall, then starts an invincible high-speed dash on leaving the wall while continuously spraying Brimstone behind.**

Grants flight.

Stay close to a wall for about **2 seconds** to finish charging; leaving early decays the charge quickly.

Leave the wall at full charge for a short kick-off into about **2 seconds** of high-speed dash:

- Invincible during the dash
- Movement input can gradually steer, but cannot snap reverse
- Continuously sprays blood Brimstone behind, dealing **30%** of the character's damage
- Hitting a wall bounces and keeps the current dash going
