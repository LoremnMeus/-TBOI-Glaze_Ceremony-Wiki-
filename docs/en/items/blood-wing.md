## Effects

**Blood Wing grants flight; after a wall charge, leaving the wall starts a steerable high-speed glide with blood Brimstone always spraying behind your path.**

- {{Flight}} Grants flight.
- Stay close to a wall for about **2 seconds** to finish charging; leaving early decays the charge quickly.
- Leave the wall at full charge for a short kick-off into about **2 seconds** of invincible dash.
- Movement input can gradually steer the dash, but cannot snap 180°.
- During the dash, blood Brimstone continuously sprays behind you, dealing **30%** damage.
- Hitting a wall bounces you and continues the dash.
