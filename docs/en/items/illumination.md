## Effects

**Illumination is a single-use active. Confirm a direction, and it opens Red Rooms in a continuous line that way.**

The first use lifts it overhead without consuming it yet.

Then:

- Press a fire direction to confirm and consume Illumination;
- Use the active again to cancel, put it away, and keep the item.

After confirm, Red Rooms open from the current room along that direction until the path can go no farther.

## Tips

You do not have to commit the moment it is raised.

If the room or chosen direction looks wrong, cancel with the active again, then reuse from another room.
