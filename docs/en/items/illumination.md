## Effects

**Illumination is a single-use Red Key tool. Pick a direction, and it opens as many Red Rooms as it can along that line.**

The first use lifts it overhead without consuming it yet.

Then:

- Press a fire direction to confirm and consume Illumination;
- Press the active again to cancel, put it away, and keep the item.

After confirm, Red Rooms open from the current room along that direction until the path can extend no farther.

## Tips

You do not have to commit the moment it is raised.

If the standing spot or chosen direction looks wrong, cancel with the active again, then reuse from a better room.
