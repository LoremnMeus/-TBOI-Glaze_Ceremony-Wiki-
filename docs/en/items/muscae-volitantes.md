## Effects

**Muscae Volitantes summons a swarm of colorful flies and randomly leaves rainbow flies, a trinket, or an item.**

| Chance | Result |
| ---: | --- |
| 66% | Leave **2–3** rainbow flies |
| 33% | Leave a random trinket |
| 1% | Leave a random item |

## Synergies

### {{Collectible:34}}

Fly rewards become War Locusts; gathering flies can also damage enemies on their path.

### {{Collectible:584}}

Fly rewards become a rainbow wisp.
