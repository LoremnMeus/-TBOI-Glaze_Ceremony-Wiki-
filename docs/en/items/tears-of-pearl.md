## Effects

Tears of Pearl can turn tears into pearl tears that linger on the ground, can be kicked, and on contact swallow enemy shots then fire a friendly tear back the other way.

## Pearl tears

Fire chance scales with luck—roughly ~5% at low luck, approaching ~15% at high luck.

## Landing and kicking

Pearls stay near the ground instead of vanishing like normal tears. Moving close kicks them, so pearls can be pushed into dense projectile fields.

## Reflect

When an enemy projectile enters about 20 units of a pearl: the projectile is removed, a friendly tear spawns going the opposite way, and the pearl spends one durability hit.

## Durability

Each pearl endures about 5 relevant collisions / reflects. After that it loses bounce-like endurance and is not a permanent shield.
