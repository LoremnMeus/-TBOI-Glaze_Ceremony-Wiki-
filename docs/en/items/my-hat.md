## Effects

Qing's Hat is both a projectile shield that follows the character and a throwable cover for dangerous enemies.

While nearby, the hat leans slightly toward the current firing direction. Only enemy projectiles that physically touch the hat are destroyed, so it does not protect the character's entire body.

## Throwing the hat

Fire in one direction for about 0.8 seconds, then release that direction to throw the hat. The hat grows slightly when fully charged.

On hitting an enemy, the hat covers it and reduces its speed to about 60%. Normal enemies remain covered for 3 seconds and bosses for 1.5 seconds. The hat returns automatically when the duration ends or the target dies.

The hat cannot block projectiles from the moment it is thrown until it reaches the character again. A missed throw also returns after hitting a wall or traveling too far.

## Tips

Keeping the hat nearby helps when crossing dense projectile patterns. Throwing it can suppress a fast enemy, but creates a temporary gap in that defense.
