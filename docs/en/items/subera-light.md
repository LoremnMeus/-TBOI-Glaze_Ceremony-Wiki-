## Effects

**Subera Light surrounds Isaac with auto-aiming laser emitters that fire together after a charge.**

The first copy creates **6** emitters. Hold fire to charge, then release: every emitter fires at once for about **30%** damage each, preferring nearby targets.

## Extra copies

Each extra copy adds **+2** emitters.

## Synergies

### {{Seija}}

First copy has only **1** emitter; each extra copy adds **+1**.
