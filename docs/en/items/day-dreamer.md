## Effects

At the start of each floor, Isaac can daydream in the starting room and temporarily gain the effect of a quality 4 item.

1. Stand completely still in the starting room for about five seconds.
2. Browse and choose a quality 4 item in the dream.
3. After confirming the dream, wait about 60 seconds. Its effect then lands on Isaac and lasts for the rest of the floor.

Press Drop to confirm the current dream early. This does not skip the following wait.

## Synergies

- Dream Catcher changes how the dream selection works.
- PJs lets Isaac fall asleep sooner.
- Blanket refreshes the dream candidates faster.
