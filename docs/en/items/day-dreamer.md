## Effects

**At the start of each floor, if you have not left the starting room yet, standing still for about 5 seconds puts you to sleep.**

The dream first shows one quality **4** passive candidate. Once it is stably displayed, press the active-item button to discard it and wait for the next; after a discard, the next candidate takes about **4–8** seconds to appear.

The chosen item does not apply immediately. The dream lasts up to about **60** seconds; on waking, you gain the current pick's effect until the floor ends. If you leave the starting room without falling asleep, you cannot dream again on that floor.

### Ending the dream

- By default you must wait about **60** seconds; **without PJs or Blanket, the drop button cannot end the dream early.**
- Movement or other input resets the sleep timer; stand still in the starting room before exploring.

## Synergies

### {{Collectible:566}}

Enter a freely movable dream: use directions to browse quality 4 candidates that keep appearing nearby, approach one to lock it, then press drop to wake and keep the locked pick. Ending with nothing locked grants no temporary item for the floor.

### {{Collectible:428}}

Greatly speeds falling asleep, and allows ending a normal dream early with drop.

### {{Collectible:535}}

Halves candidate refresh wait, and allows ending a normal dream early with drop.

### {{Seija}}

Dreams quality **0** items instead.

## Notes

- In a normal dream, the active-item button **rerolls the candidate**—it is not shoot-to-cycle.
- Early-end from PJs / Blanket applies to the normal dream; Dream Catcher's dream can end with drop on its own.

## Trivia

- Day Dreamer is one of this mod's **rainbow** items.
- Its sprite is a recolor of vanilla **Depression**, keeping that item's cloud shape.
- Clouds and daydreams share a visual link: dream or fantasy scenes are often shown floating in clouds, and this item's look follows that image.
