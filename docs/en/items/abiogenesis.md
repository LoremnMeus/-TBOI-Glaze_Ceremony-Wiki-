## Effects

**Abiogenesis is a four-resource zeroing experiment: pay charge, coin, key, and bomb once each; leftover resources fail the trial, and a perfect zero proves life from nothing.**

- Each use spends **1** charge plus 1 {{Coin}} {{Key}} {{Bomb}} (unless excluded)
- On failure: spawn 1 pickup of the most remaining resource (ties randomize)
- {{Warning}} Golden Key / Golden Bomb still count even if normal counts are 0
- On success: all four observations are 0 → remove the active, record a proof, spawn 1 Abiogenesis familiar; prove again for more familiars

## Synergies

### {{Collectible:34}}

Failures exclude one non-zero resource from later payments and zero checks.

### {{Collectible:584}}

On failure, spawn a wisp matching the most remaining resource.
