## Effects

**Nihilistic Artificial Eye spends part of its value up front as a four-way choice; if it is still taken, a smaller immediate reward buys later chances to appear again.**

When it appears, it also spawns **3 random collectibles**, forming a four-way choice with itself.

So even if it is not chosen, the run has already gained the benefit of seeing three extra items.

If it is chosen, the pickup grants:

- {{Damage}} **+0.33** Damage
- **2 chances** for later spawned collectibles to be replaced by Nihilistic Artificial Eye

## Appearing again

While chances remain, each later collectible spawn has a **10%** chance to be replaced by Nihilistic Artificial Eye.

A chance is spent only when a replacement actually happens, so the two chances do not automatically expire after the next two spawns.
