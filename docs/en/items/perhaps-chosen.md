## Effects

Perhaps Chosen is an item choice that does not disappear when rejected.

Taking another item from the same choice group or leaving without taking anything both count as not choosing it. It rejoins the next suitable item choice, including a new opportunity that appears later in the same room.

Only taking the choice slot occupied by Perhaps Chosen ends that unresolved choice.

Perhaps Chosen itself has no additional effect once collected.

## Choice slots and rerolls

Perhaps Chosen follows a choice slot, not the collectible currently displayed on its pedestal.

For example:

**A + Perhaps Chosen**

Using {{Collectible:105}} may turn it into:

**B + (C / Perhaps Chosen)**

- Taking B leaves Perhaps Chosen unchosen, so it keeps waiting.
- Taking the right-hand pedestal grants its currently displayed item and ends that Perhaps Chosen.

Therefore, even while the right-hand pedestal displays C instead of Perhaps Chosen, taking it ends the matching unresolved choice as long as it remains that choice slot.

Other effects that make one pedestal cycle between several items follow the same rule.

## Synergies

### {{Seija}}

With the Seija enhancement, whenever Perhaps Chosen rejoins an item choice, its choice slot gains one random item from the current item pool as an additional cycling option.

This does not change the choice-slot rule: taking any item currently displayed by that cycling pedestal ends the matching Perhaps Chosen.

## Notes

- Multiple unresolved Perhaps Chosen instances remain independent and may join the same item choice at once.
- Perhaps Chosen may join a choice that already contains another Perhaps Chosen.
- A rejected shop copy retains its actual price; rerolling it into another item does not change that price.
- An ordinary item that already has a price cannot receive a waiting Perhaps Chosen.

## Trivia

The flavor description "Please, look again." quotes related dialogue from *The Fifth Republic*.

Aside from that flavor line, the item's name and mechanics are not based on that work.
