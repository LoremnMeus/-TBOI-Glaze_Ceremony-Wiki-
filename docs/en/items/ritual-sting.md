## Effects

**Ritual Sting lets you sacrifice held collectibles to charge one of six color bars; crossing thresholds grants ongoing effects.**

On use:

1. Choose which color to charge;
2. Select one held collectible to sacrifice;
3. That collectible's color determines how much progress the chosen bar gains.

Matching the item's primary color usually grants full value; choosing an adjacent color usually grants partial value.

The UI previews how much the selected item would add to your chosen color.

Defeating champions also adds a small amount to their matching color.

Clearing a room drains all six colors by **3%**.

Each color starts at **100%**. Key thresholds: **0%**, **below 50%**, **above 90%**, **above 150%**, **above 250%** (not every color has every tier).

**All thresholds you meet apply at once.** For example, red at 160% grants both the >90% and >150% effects.
