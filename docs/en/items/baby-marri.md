## Mechanics

## Effects

**Baby Marri switches between Angel and Devil forms.**

Starts in **Angel form**:

* Angel Room conversion chance **+15%**.

Taking damage switches to **Devil form**:

* Devil Room chance **+15%**.

Each subsequent hit switches to the other form.

## Angel form

Angel form does not increase the overall chance for a Devil Room to appear. Instead, it increases the chance for a Devil Room to be converted into an Angel Room.

## Devil form

Devil form directly increases the chance for a Devil Room to appear.

## Extra copies

Each additional copy of Baby Marri adds another **15%** to the bonus of the current form.

All copies owned by the same player share the same form and switch together when the player takes damage.
