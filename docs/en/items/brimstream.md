## Mechanics

<!-- Manual body. The exporter never overwrites this file once it exists. -->

Spawns a brimstone rocket that repeatedly crosses the battlefield. It leaves the room, chooses a fixed straight route through the enemy cluster, warns at the entry point, accelerates in, and exits through the far side. It does not home after committing to a route.

Direct contact deals heavy damage without stopping the rocket. Its wake remains for about **1.5 seconds** and periodically damages enemies; trails from multiple rockets may overlap. With no enemies present, the rocket loosely orbits the player without leaving a damaging trail.

{{Collectible:118}} makes the trail wider, brighter, and stronger. The corresponding {{Collectible:706}} locust also leaves a shorter trail while flying quickly.
