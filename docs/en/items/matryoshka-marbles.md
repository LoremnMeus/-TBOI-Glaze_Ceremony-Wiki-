## Mechanics

## Effects

**Fired tears have a 20% chance to become Matryoshka Marbles. On hitting an enemy or obstacle, they bounce and split into two smaller marbles.**

- After splitting, marbles are pulled by gravity along the original fire direction, forming curved bounce paths.
- Each marble can split up to **3** times in a row, and the resulting marbles grow smaller.
- With multi-shot, each tear rolls separately.

## Special weapons

Matryoshka Marbles can also be fired by other weapons:

- **{{Collectible:118}}:** Each attack has a **20%** chance to fire **4** marbles in a row.
- **{{Collectible:68}}, {{Collectible:395}}:** Each attack has a **20%** chance to fire one extra marble.
- **{{Collectible:114}}:** Throws have a chance to fire an extra marble.
- **{{Collectible:329}}:** Each attack cycle has a **20%** chance to enlarge the floating tear and spawn two smaller marbles on impact.

Marbles from the floating tear curve toward the collision target, and the floating tear itself is not consumed.

## Trivia

- The name and art come from Matryorbshka in Peglin.
- In Peglin, Matryorbshka splits into smaller orbs each time it hits a peg. This item keeps that idea by letting marbles split and bounce off enemies, echoing Peglin's peg-bounce play.
