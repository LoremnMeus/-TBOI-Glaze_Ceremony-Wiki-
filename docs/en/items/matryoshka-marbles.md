## Mechanics

## Effects

Matryoshka marbles split into two smaller marbles on impact. Each split first knocks the marbles out to either side, then pulls them along a fixed gravity, so their paths curve.

Normal tears have a 20% chance to become matryoshka marbles. With multi-shot, each tear rolls separately.

Each marble splits at most 3 times. The last marbles still deal damage, but they do not split. Later marbles inherit the first marble's gravity direction.

## Synergies

Matryoshka marbles can also come from some special attacks.

- **{{Collectible:118}}:** Each attack has a 20% chance to fire 4 marbles in a row, with slightly different directions and speeds.
- **{{Collectible:68}}, {{Collectible:395}}:** Each attack has a 20% chance to fire 1 extra marble.
- **{{Collectible:114}}:** A throw has a chance to fire an extra marble.
- **{{Collectible:329}}:** Each attack cycle has a 20% chance to temporarily turn the floating tear into a matryoshka marble. While triggered, the floating tear grows larger and spawns 2 smaller marbles on its first impact.

The floating tear does not disappear when it spawns marbles. Each cycle rolls again, and a cycle that does not trigger restores the previous appearance.

Marbles spawned by the floating tear curve toward the center of the target they hit, instead of falling along the usual firing direction.

## Notes

A marble's gravity direction is chosen when it is created and does not change as the marble moves. Marbles shrink as they split.

If a special attack also fires normal tears that can become marbles, it does not also fire the extra marbles from the special weapon.
