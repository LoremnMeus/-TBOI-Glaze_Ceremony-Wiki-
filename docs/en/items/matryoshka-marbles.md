## 机制说明

## Effect

**Quality:** 2

**Item pool:** Treasure Room

Tears have a chance to become matryoshka marbles. Each tear from multi-shot or scatter fires rolls on its own.

A marble bounces when it hits an enemy and splits into two smaller marbles. A head-on hit sends them back at an angle. A glancing hit sends them along the contact. They then curve back toward the original firing direction.

Marbles split at most three times. The last generation still deals damage and does not split. Walls, rocks, and friendly targets do not cause a split.

## Movement

Every child keeps the first marble's firing direction as its gravity. Later hits do not replace that direction with the child's current movement.

The sprite turns to follow the marble's actual movement, and each generation is smaller.

## Other weapons

Brimstone, Technology, and Tech X each get one chance per attack to fire an extra matryoshka marble. An attack that already fired a tear eligible for the marble does not also gain this extra marble.

Mom's Knife, Spirit Sword, Bone Club, Ludovico Technique, Epic Fetus, and familiars do not gain marbles from this item.
