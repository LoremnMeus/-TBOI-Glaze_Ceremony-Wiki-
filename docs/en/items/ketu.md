## Mechanics

## Effects

**Ketu travels clockwise along the inner HUD track. As it passes different HUD regions, it briefly grants matching bonuses.**

- **Stats:** Raise the matching speed, tears, damage, range, shot speed, or luck.
- **Resources:** Temporarily add matching coins, bombs, or keys.
- **Hearts:** Briefly grant invulnerability.
- **Map:** Reveal the map.
- **Devil and Angel room chance:** Raise the matching room chance.
- **Active item:** Gain **4** extra temporary charge bars.
- **Passive items:** Briefly apply **1** extra copy of the matching item.
- **Trinkets:** Briefly apply **1** extra copy of the matching trinket.

When it passes an item or trinket, that icon turns gold. After Ketu leaves, the bonus lingers briefly, then ends.

## Synergies

### Zodiac items

Each held Zodiac item becomes a companion that follows Ketu.

Companions can also trigger bonuses along the track.

### {{Item:Rahu}}

While both Ketu and Rahu are held, the two mains run the inner and outer tracks.

When they meet, nearby companions may transfer to the other main's orbit and trigger that orbit's effects.

### {{Seija}}

While Ketu occupies unused leftover space along the top of the inner HUD track, the room speeds up and the run timer advances extra; Ketu also moves much more slowly there. Leaving the time region restores normal room speed immediately.

## Trivia

- “Ketu” comes from Indian astronomy and mythology, where Ketu and Rahu form a paired celestial concept.
- The item also draws on the Yu-Gi-Oh! Dracotail Spell Card “Ketu Dracotail” (计都星辰). The companion-star link with Zodiac items was inspired by that series as well.
