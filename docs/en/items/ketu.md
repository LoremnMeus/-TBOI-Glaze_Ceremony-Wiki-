## Mechanics

## Effects

**Ketu travels clockwise along the inner HUD track. While it passes different on-screen regions, it briefly grants the player matching bonuses. Stats, resource counters, hearts, and the map can all become targets.**

What Ketu grants depends on where it currently is:

- **Stat regions**: Temporarily raise the matching stat (speed, tears, damage, range, shot speed, or luck).
- **Resource regions**: Temporarily refill the matching coins, bombs, or keys up to the current hold cap.
- **Heart region**: Briefly grant invulnerability.
- **Map region**: Briefly reveal the map.
- **Deal-chance regions**: Raise the matching Devil or Angel room chance.

After Ketu leaves a region, the bonus lingers briefly, then ends.

## Notes

Ketu follows HUD layout positions, not the player's room position. It does not keep granting a bonus while it is away from that region.

Through blank stretches of the track, Ketu moves faster.

Companion stars are visual only: held Zodiac items are not removed from the player.

## Synergies

### Zodiac items

Each held Zodiac item creates a matching companion that trails Ketu on the same track.

Companions can also trigger region effects, so several Zodiacs can keep different bonuses active in sequence.

### {{Item:Rahu}}

While both Ketu and Rahu are held, the two mains run the inner and outer tracks.

When they meet, companions near the meeting point may switch to the other main's orbit and then trigger that orbit's effects.
