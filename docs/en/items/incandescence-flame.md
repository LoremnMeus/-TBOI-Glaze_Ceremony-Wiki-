## Mechanics

## Effects

While spraying, Incandescence Flame produces a continuous stream of fire that extends outward from the player's current position and aim. Moving or changing aim redirects where new flames are emitted, while previously emitted flames remain in the room and gradually dissipate.

Each enemy can be ignited only once during a spray. A subsequent spray can ignite that enemy again.

When spraying stops, the existing flames continue to deal direct damage until they dissipate completely. The next charge can begin only after the lingering flames are gone.

## Notes

- Leaving a room clears the active stream and lingering flames.
- Leaving while spraying or while lingering flames remain resets the charge.
- Charge progress is preserved when leaving during charging or after reaching full charge but before releasing to spray.
