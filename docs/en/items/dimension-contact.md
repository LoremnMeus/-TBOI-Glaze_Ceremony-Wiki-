## Effects

**Dimension Contact moves a small part of a future room's battle into the present.**

On use, it pulls 2–3 ordinary enemies from unexplored rooms into the current room and closes the doors. Once defeated here, those enemies will not appear again when their original rooms are reached.

The item relocates existing enemies rather than duplicating them. Certain special enemies cannot be selected.

## Synergies

- Book of Virtues creates a wisp with a teleportation effect.
