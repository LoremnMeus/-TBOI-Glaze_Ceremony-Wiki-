## Effects

**Moment recovers residual energy left by attacks and gradually turns it into stat growth.**

Attacks can leave residual energy when they end, hit, or continue dealing damage. The energy flies back to the character and randomly increases damage, tears, range, speed, or luck. Repeated growth in the same stat has diminishing returns.

Above **300** total energy, the Morning Star Device begins venting stored energy and the attached bonuses. If energy still exceeds **999**, Zero Reversal begins: accumulated bonuses apply in reverse, while the character and room appear vertically inverted. Venting continues until energy falls below 3, ending the reversal and restoring normal behavior.

## Notes

- Residual energy deals a small amount of contact damage, but its main purpose is returning to the character and granting a stat unit.
- Ordinary attacks stop producing new residual energy during Zero Reversal.
- Stat growth has diminishing returns; every recovered unit does not produce the same visible increase.

## Tips

Near overload, rapidly collecting more energy can cross the Zero Reversal threshold. Allow the Morning Star Device time to vent if stable bonuses matter more than continued growth.

## Synergies

### {{Seija}}

Under Seija, the Morning Star Device cannot vent energy. Bonuses can keep accumulating, but Zero Reversal will no longer end through natural venting once triggered.
