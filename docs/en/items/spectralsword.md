## Effects

**An oni dwells in Spectral Sword. When you raise the blade, it seizes the sword and reshapes your stats and resources under your direction: to gain something, you must cut something else from yourself.**

Use the 0-charge active to open the reshape panel. It will not open while active enemies are present.

## Stat Reshape

- Lowering a stat releases **Spirit**; raising another stat spends it.
- A small starting Spirit budget allows limited adjustment immediately.
- Each stat has an adjustment range, and the panel previews the resulting values.

## Resource Conversion

A separate page offers discrete trades (coins ↔ bombs/keys, coins → half soul, etc.), applied immediately with no debt.

## Confirm and Reset

- Panel changes are previews until **Confirm** is selected.
- **Reset** removes every stat adjustment made by the sword and restores its starting Spirit budget.

## Notes

- Losing the item disables modifiers immediately; reclaiming in the same run can restore the prior plan.

## Versus Squiresaga

Spectral Sword edits **you**; {{QingCollectible:Squiresaga}} edits **the world**.
