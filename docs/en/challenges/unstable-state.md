## Mechanics

## Gameplay

When Eden takes damage from an enemy, up to **3** random passives or familiars drop to the floor. They can be picked up again; quest and hidden items do not drop this way.

Ordinary enemies may steal the dropped items and become stronger. Kill a thief to recover its item. Bosses cannot steal them.
