## Mechanics

## Gameplay

Unstable State makes Eden shed build pieces on hit: random passives drop and must be reclaimed before normal enemies steal them.

## Rules

- Enemy damage knocks out up to **3** random passives/familiars (quest/hidden excluded); touch to reclaim.
- Non-Boss enemies can steal dropped items and grow stronger; kill them to recover.
- Bosses do not steal dropped items.
