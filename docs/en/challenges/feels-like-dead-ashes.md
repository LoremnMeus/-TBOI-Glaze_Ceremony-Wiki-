## Mechanics

## Gameplay

Feels Like Dead Ashes rewrites Tainted Isaac builds: normal passives do not work while held. Only {{Item:ember}} keeps the last permanently lost passive effect. The run is about which effects Embers should remember, not how many items you hold.

## {{Item:ember}}

The challenge starts with **8** Embers that do not occupy Tainted Isaac's normal item slots. When a copyable passive (or familiar) is permanently lost, Embers retarget to that effect; all Embers share the same “last lost” target.

Full Ember rules: {{Item:ember}}.

## Item rules

- Non-Ember passives and familiars are disabled (quest-style exceptions follow the implementation).
- Actives, trinkets, cards, and pills are outside this disable path.
- Growth happens after permanent loss, when Embers take over—not while the passive sits unused on the character.
