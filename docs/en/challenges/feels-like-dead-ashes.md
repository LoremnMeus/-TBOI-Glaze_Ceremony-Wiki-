## Mechanics

## Gameplay

All eight item slots begin filled with {{Item:ember}}. Other passives and familiars can occupy a slot, but their own effects stay inactive. The build instead relies on Embers copying the most recently permanently lost item.

## {{Item:ember}}

Each Ember copies the same most recently lost eligible item. Picking up a new passive does not change the copy target; it must leave your inventory permanently first.

Discarding an Ember frees a slot for another candidate, but reduces the number of copies. Active items, trinkets, cards and pills still work normally. For the item's full rules, see {{Item:ember}}.
