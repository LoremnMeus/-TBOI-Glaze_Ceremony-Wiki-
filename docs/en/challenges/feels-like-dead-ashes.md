## Mechanics

## Gameplay

Feels Like Dead Ashes turns Tainted Isaac into a slot-for-multiplier trade: all eight slots start filled with {{Item:ember}}. Normal passives can sit in slots as candidates, but they do nothing while held—only Embers copy the last permanently lost item.

## {{Item:ember}}

At the start of the challenge, Tainted Isaac's 8 item slots are all occupied by Embers. Embers themselves occupy slots normally.  
Each Ember you currently hold copies the most recently permanently lost copyable passive.  
Other passives and familiars can occupy slots, but their own effects stay disabled.  
Discarding Embers frees slots for more candidate items, and permanently lowers the current copy multiplier.

Discarding an Ember only lowers the multiplier; it does not change the copy target. Picking up a normal passive also does not retarget Embers—that only happens after the passive is permanently lost.

Full Ember rules: {{Item:ember}}.

## Item rules

- Non-Ember passives and familiars are disabled (quest-style exceptions follow the implementation).
- Actives, trinkets, cards, and pills are outside this disable path.
- Growth happens after permanent loss, when Embers take over—not while the passive sits unused on the character.
