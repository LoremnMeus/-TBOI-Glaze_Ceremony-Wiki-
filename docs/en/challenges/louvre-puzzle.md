## Gameplay

Each floor begins in a Death Certificate gallery. Its pedestals continually change items, and change faster as you approach them.

Use the pocket-slot Pause to stop every pedestal for about **2 seconds** while you choose an item. Breakfast pedestals that appear during the changes cannot be picked up.
