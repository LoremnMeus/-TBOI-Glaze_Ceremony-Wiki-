## Mechanics

## Gameplay

Anna's palm black hole destroys enemies it fully absorbs, including Bosses, instead of storing them. It can still swallow some terrain and obstacles.

No floor pickups can be collected, including item pedestals. Each new floor grants **2 Black Hearts, 1 bomb and 1 key** in addition to what you already have.
