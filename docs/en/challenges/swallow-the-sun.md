## Mechanics

## Gameplay

Normal {{Character:anna}} stores sucked enemies in her palm black hole to release later. Swallow the Sun **auto-digests** fully sucked enemies (Bosses included) instead.

The tradeoff: no floor pickups or pedestals for normal resource/item growth.

## Palm black hole

- Normal enemies must finish the full suction process; brushing the hole is not enough.
- Bosses can be fully sucked and digested.
- Some terrain and obstacles can be swallowed.

## Resources

All floor pickups (including pedestals) cannot be collected. Each new floor **grants** 2 Black Hearts, 1 key, and 1 bomb (additive, not a restore-to-fixed-value).

## Notes

Despite the growth ban, auto-digesting enemies and Bosses sharply lowers combat pressure, which is why the challenge is marked Easy.
