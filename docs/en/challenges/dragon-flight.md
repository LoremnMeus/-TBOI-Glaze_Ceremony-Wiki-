## Gameplay

Normal shooting is disabled. Use {{Item:how-to-fly}} to attack in flight: its tears linger along your path before slowly falling. Where you fly determines where those tears land.

Tears briefly hold their height near the floor. To keep hitting enemies, steer your flight path across them.
