## Mechanics

## Gameplay

Mouse clicks replace normal shooting. Each left-click damages enemies within about 30 of the cursor for **50%** of your current {{Damage}}. Holding the button will not repeat the attack.

Clicking a grid tile also deals 1 damage to obstacles such as poop and fires. The challenge attempts to enable mouse control at the start; there is no equivalent controller input.
