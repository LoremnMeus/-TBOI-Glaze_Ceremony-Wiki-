## Mechanics

## Gameplay

Cookie Clicker replaces normal shooting with mouse-click attacks. Damage is dealt by clicking enemy positions, not by holding a fire direction.

## Rules

- Each left-click damages hurt-able enemies within about 30 of the cursor for **50%** of current {{Damage}}.
- Holding the button does not repeat; one press = one hit.
- Clicking grids deals 1 grid damage (poop, fires, etc.).
- Mouse-only: there is no controller substitute click; the run tries to enable mouse control on start.
