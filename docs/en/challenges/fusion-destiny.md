## Mechanics

## Gameplay

Fusion Destiny turns Tainted Cain's crafting into the combat loop: no normal shooting—use the Bag of Crafting to capture enemies into basic pickups, then craft items from those ingredients.

Treasure Rooms and Shops do not generate; other room types still appear by their usual rules.

## Capturing enemies

Swinging the bag into a hurt-able enemy:

- Normal enemies convert into basic pickups and are removed;
- Hits resolve near the bag tip during the swing.

## Bosses

Bosses are not one-shot converted away. Bag hits still grant pickups and knock extra ingredients out of the Boss while the fight continues.

## Limits

No Treasure Rooms or Shops.
