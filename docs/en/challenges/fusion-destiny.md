## Gameplay

Normal shooting is disabled. Swing the Bag of Crafting to capture enemies and collect ingredients for crafting items. An ordinary enemy touched by the bag tip disappears and leaves pickups behind.

The bag cannot remove a Boss outright, but each capture still yields ingredients from it. Treasure Rooms and Shops do not generate in this challenge.
