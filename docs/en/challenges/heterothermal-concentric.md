## Mechanics

## Gameplay

Heterothermal Concentric forces Jacob & Esau to shoot toward each other; tears that pass through the partner are boosted.

## Rules

- Fire direction is locked toward the other character—positioning is the aim.
- Tears that pass near the partner gain damage and speed; standing too close disables the boost.
