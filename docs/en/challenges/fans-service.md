## Mechanics

## Gameplay

Fans Service turns clears into managing a fan army: walking near normal enemies permanently charms them as allies. Boss rooms force a mass “unfan.”

## Core rules

- Non-Boss enemies in range are permanently charmed and grow slightly after room clears.
- Charmed fans persist across rooms and resist normal player damage.
- The **first** Boss room each floor removes all charm; Super Satan's room always does.
- Some special enemies (including Super Satan-related) cannot be charmed.
