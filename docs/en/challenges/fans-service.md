## Mechanics

## Gameplay

Approaching ordinary enemies charms them into allies that follow you between rooms. Your normal attacks cannot hurt them, and their health rises slightly after each room clear.

## Boss Rooms

On the first visit to a Boss Room each floor, your allies lose their charm and turn hostile again. This always happens in Super Satan's room. Bosses and some special enemies cannot be charmed.
