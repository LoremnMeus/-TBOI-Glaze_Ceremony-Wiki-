## Gameplay

Hold an attack direction to charge the train. Once the bar fills, it launches in that direction, entering from behind the character and accelerating across the room. It hits enemies and breaks destructible obstacles along its path.

If the bar is full and you stop attacking, the charge remains until you launch. For the train's other effects, see {{Item:hyper-velocity}}.
