## Mechanics

## Gameplay

Safe Driving replaces normal shots with a charge-then-launch high-speed train that rams through the room.

## Rules

- A dedicated charge bar fills under valid attack control.
- When full, an attack direction launches the train; charging then restarts.
- The train reuses {{Item:hyper-velocity}} Harmony: enters from behind, accelerates, hits enemies, and breaks breakable terrain.
- A full bar without a fire direction does not waste the charge.
