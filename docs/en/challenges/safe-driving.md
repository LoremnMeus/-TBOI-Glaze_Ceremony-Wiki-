## Mechanics

## Effects

**Safe Driving replaces normal shooting with a charged high-speed train: fill the meter, then fire in your attack direction so the train rams across the room.**

- A dedicated charge bar is shown on screen.
- Once full, providing an attack direction launches the train; afterward it charges again.
- The train reuses {{Item:hyper-velocity}} Harmony: it enters from behind along the chosen direction, keeps accelerating, rams enemies, and destroys breakable terrain along the way.

## Notes

- If the meter is full but you are not holding an attack direction, the charge is not wasted.

<details>
<summary>Technical details</summary>

While attack controls can work, charge builds each frame; at **100** with a valid attack direction the train fires and the counter resets.

</details>
