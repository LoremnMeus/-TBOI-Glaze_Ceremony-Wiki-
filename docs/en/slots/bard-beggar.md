## Mechanics

## Effects

The Bard Beggar writes one resource offering into a blessing that plays only on the **next floor**. Payment is immediate; the blessing covers that entire floor and ends on leaving it. One song per encounter.

| Offer | Song | Next floor |
| --- | --- | --- |
| {{Coin}} 5 coins | Song of Plenty | Shop coin prices are 2 lower |
| {{Key}} 1 key | Song of the Road | Reveals the shortest route to the Treasure Room and keeps the doors along it open |
| {{Bomb}} 1 bomb | War Song | The first enemy death in each room causes an explosion |
| {{Heart}} 1 heart | Requiem | Generated red hearts have a 25% chance to become soul hearts |

## How to trade

Approach to raise the current offer.

- Left/right to switch;
- Walk in to confirm;
- Drop / RT to cancel;
- Unaffordable offers do not appear.

## Extra rules

### {{Coin}} Song of Plenty

The discount is recalculated after rerolls and Restock without stacking repeatedly. Prices never fall below 1¢; free goods and non-coin prices are unchanged.

### {{Key}} Song of the Road

The route uses only rooms that are already connected on the floor and never creates rooms. With multiple treasure rooms, it chooses a shortest reachable route from the starting room. Route doors reopen whenever their room is entered.

### {{Bomb}} War Song

The explosion triggers on the first true enemy death in each room. Leaving and re-entering that room does not restore the trigger; friendly units and non-enemies do not count.

### {{Heart}} Requiem

A full red heart maps to a full soul heart, and a half red heart maps to a half soul heart. Special hearts are unchanged, and each eligible heart is rolled only once. Payment prefers 1 red heart, else 1 soul heart.

## Appearance

About half the time on first entry into a Secret Exit room. Leaves after one gift.
