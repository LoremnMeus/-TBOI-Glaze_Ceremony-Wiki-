## Mechanics

### Effect
Walk near to lift a gift you can actually pay. Switch with shoot left/right, walk in to confirm. Drop/RT cancels.

Unavailable resources are omitted, not shown as unaffordable.

- {{Coin}} **Song of Plenty**: pay 5¢. First shop buy next floor refunds 8–12¢.
- {{Key}} **Song of the Road**: pay 1 key. Reveal {{TreasureRoom}} next floor.
- {{Bomb}} **War Song**: pay 1 bomb. {{Damage}} +1 in the first 3 enemy rooms next floor.
- {{Heart}} **Requiem**: pay 1 heart. Block the first hit next floor.

The blessing lasts one floor. Can spawn in the special exit.

### Notes
Static EID is only the controls. Dynamic EID describes the gift currently held.
