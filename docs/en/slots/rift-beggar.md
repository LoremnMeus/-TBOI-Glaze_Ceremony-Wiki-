## Mechanics

The Rift beggar pulls in and swallows nearby basic pickups. It does not require the player to walk into it to pay.

### How to use

Leave coins, bombs, keys, hearts, cards, pills, and other basic pickups near it; each pickup that enters the rift counts as 1 payment. Collectible pedestals are not swallowed.

How many more payments are still needed is shown on the entity’s description. Leaving the room or saving and continuing the current run does not reset payment progress.

### Reward

After swallowing 25 basic pickups in total, the Rift beggar spawns 1 quality-4 collectible, then teleports away. The reward prefers quality-4 items the party does not already hold.

### Appearance

While playing {{Character:anna}}, the Rift beggar appears on even floors. After Anna gains the effect of {{Collectible:619}}, it can appear on every floor.

### Notes

- It only swallows real pickups on the floor; it does not read inventory already stored in Anna’s handheld black hole.
- When blown up it drops only 1 Black Heart and does not refund pickups already paid in.

### Tips

Near 25 payments, dump low-value pickups nearby so you do not burn important resources on unfinished progress.
