## Mechanics

### Playstyle

**Impale enemies on the spear, then tear them apart with a high-speed tip.**

{{Character:tecro}} does not fire ordinary tears. He controls a spear that stays in orbit around him. Fire direction moves the tip; a thrust that hits a valid enemy can pin them to the weapon and keep them there while it moves.

**Having an enemy on the spear does not end the attack.** Keep spinning so the tip stays fast—it keeps damaging impaled targets and anything else it sweeps through.

### First time with Tecro

1. **Spin the spear.** Use fire direction to move the tip around Tecro.
2. **Impale.** Charge, then thrust so the tip hits; pierceable enemies lock onto the spear.
3. **Keep spinning.** Faster tip motion means higher sustained damage. Do not leave the weapon parked on a foe.
4. **Thrust again.** Chase, pierce several targets, or put the tip back on a better orbit.

Middle mouse toggles mouse aiming.

### Impaling enemies

When the spear hits a hostile during a valid thrust window, it locks them on. This is not a random roll: if the target can be pierced, is not held by another effect, and is not in the short immunity after escaping, a thrust hit pins them.

Bosses can be impaled too, but usually escape sooner. The first thrust tries to pin suitable enemies along the path; after something is already impaled, whether more targets can be added depends on Tecro’s current pierce capacity—there is no single hard cap for every attack phase.

Dead or escaped victims clear off the spear immediately; with no targets left, the spear returns to its normal state.

### Tip speed and damage

Tecro’s tip is not fixed contact damage. Collision damage scales with the tip’s real movement speed; fast sweeps or sustained spins usually beat slowly hugging a target.

Player movement and spear rotation both change tip speed. Different spear states and attack items further modify final damage, so it is not a simple linear multiplier.

### Charge and thrust

Holding fire spins the tip and builds charge; releasing extends the spear along the current tip direction. Thrusts are for quick contact, hitting several enemies at once, or sending the tip back to a good spin position.

Thrust is a burst for reshaping the fight, not Tecro’s only damage source. Enemies already on the spear keep taking damage as you keep spinning.

### Pierce capacity

Sharp enough items raise Tecro’s pierce capacity so the spear can more easily lock additional enemies while some are already pinned. Mom’s Knife, Cupid’s Arrow, Spear of Destiny, and similar items raise it for the long term; using nails, razors, scissors, and other sharp actives can also boost it for the current room.

### Trophies

Killing certain enemies with the spear for the first time records a trophy and shows exclusive text. Those records persist in the current save.

### Tips

- **Do not park the spear.** Tip speed is part of the damage.
- **Impale is only the start.** Once something is on the spear, keep spinning hard.
- **Use the whole arc.** Think about where the tip will sweep next, not only where it is now.
- **Thrust reorganizes the fight.** Chase, pierce, or put the spear back on a better path.

> Tecro does not only stab. He keeps the target on his weapon—and keeps turning.
