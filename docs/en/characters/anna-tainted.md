## Mechanics

### Playstyle

**Pick a landing point with the reticle and crash down as the weapon.**

{{Character:anna-tainted}} does not use Anna’s handheld black hole. While attacking, she aims a reticle and charges; at full charge she moves above the reticle, then drops at high speed to hit enemies near the landing. The base crash deals **5× current tear damage**; attack-style items may further change form, range, or multiplier.

**Wherever the reticle is, that is where she ends up.** Protection during the crash and a short invuln after landing can get her into danger—they do not guarantee safety once the attack ends.

### First time with annA

1. **Choose the landing.** Fire input moves the reticle and starts charging.
2. **Finish the crash.** At full charge, annA auto-moves above the reticle and drops hard.
3. **Leave the landing.** After the attack she stays put; use the remaining brief invuln to reposition. If targets are still alive, that is when things get dangerous.

### The landing is your stance

The reticle looks like long-range aiming, but annA does not attack from a safe seat. After charge completes, she herself goes above the target and finishes the crash.

So the reticle sets both the attack position and where she stands afterward. The densest pack often maximizes coverage—and is often the worst place to be when invuln ends.

### Invulnerability window

annA is protected during the crash, then gains **0.25 seconds (15 frames)** of invulnerability after the attack. When that ends, she is still on the landing.

That protection exists so the attack can finish and to give a tiny exit window. The next crash must charge again, so there is a normal damage window between attacks.

### Tips

- **Do not only read the damage.** The reticle decides where you hit and where you stand when it is over.
- **Use invuln to enter, not to linger.** The short window is for crossing the worst moment—it ends fast.
- **Bosses are not always one-shot.** Against high HP, leave movement room for the exposure between crashes.

> annA is very good at ending fights—and not always good at the part where the fight is not over yet.
