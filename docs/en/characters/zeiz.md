## Mechanics

::: warning Work in progress
Zeiz’s Control Hub, administrator candidates, and some Follies are already wired in. Proposals, the full administrator roster, and some long-term rules are still in progress. This page also records confirmed final design direction.
:::

### Playstyle

**Appoint an administrator who carries a Folly, and let that mistaken belief become the world’s rules.**

{{Character:zeiz}} does not inherit Zeis’s knowledge or temporary-item systems. At run start and after each new floor, Zeiz enters the {{System:control-hub}} and appoints one administrator from several candidates.

Each administrator has a **Folly**—a one-sided, wrong reading of the world that they treat as absolute truth. Once appointed, they manage part of the game’s rules under that reading.

Administrators are not handing out ordinary buffs. Their judgment rewrites rules that used to be obvious, and Zeiz must keep advancing as those rules stack.

### First time with Zeiz

1. **Enter the Control Hub.** At run start and after each new floor, you get a new appointment chance.
2. **Read each candidate’s Folly.** Every administrator only understands part of the world and treats that as common sense.
3. **Appoint an administrator.** Their Folly becomes one of the run’s lasting rules.
4. **Draw their Interest.** Actions they care about gradually make them notice Zeiz.
5. **Wait for a Proposal.** With enough Interest, they prepare a **Proposal** that would reshape the world further. Proposal decisions are still in development.

### Folly

A Folly is not a rule the administrator wrote on purpose—it is their mistaken understanding of the world.

They over-generalize what they know into universal law and rewrite matching systems. Keys, shops, soul and flesh, item choice, and more can each be redefined by some administrator’s habits.

Multiple administrators can be active at once, so late-run worlds are jointly managed by several incompatible readings.

> The problem is not that the administrator does not know—it is that they are sure they already do.

### Interest

Each administrator watches particular behaviors. The more Zeiz acts along those lines, the faster Interest builds.

Interest is not only a progress bar. It means the administrator is noticing that the world seems to prove their judgment. Each “confirmation” makes them more willing to push that reading further.

### Proposal

When Interest in Zeiz and the current world is high enough, the administrator no longer settles for their original scope and prepares a new **Proposal**. A Proposal tries to prove their Folly further—expanding a judgment that touched only some rules into a wider domain.

Proposals are not ordinary rewards; they demand that the world fit their understanding more tightly. How the player answers, and the full rule impact, is still in development.

### Special administrators

Not every administrator only rewrites keys, shops, or combat. Some special administrators question not a single mechanic, but **how administrators are supposed to understand and manage the world at all.**

They never appear in ordinary candidate pools. Appointment methods and full rules are still in development.

For the roster, implemented Follies, Interest numbers, and candidate rules, see {{System:control-hub}}.

### Tips

- **Do not only chase the next floor’s gain.** Follies last; think about how an appointment rewrites later floors.
- **New rules do not replace old ones.** When appointing someone new, consider how their Folly stacks with administrators already in play.
- **Playing along builds Interest faster.** The more sure they become, the harder the world may be to handle later.

> They do not think they are twisting the world—they are sure the world was always meant to be this way.
