## Mechanics

### Playstyle

**Send the knife to the enemy—then follow it.**

{{Character:w-qing}} does not use ordinary tears. He fights with knives. Up close he swings or stabs; at range he throws.

**A thrown knife is not only a projectile.** Knives lodged in enemies, pickups, or other valid targets leave a return point. On return, Qing snaps to that point and retrieves the knife. He can plant the next position with a throw, then arrive there himself to keep attacking.

### First time with Qing

1. **Attack with the knife.** Close range swings or stabs; farther range throws.
2. **Leave knives.** Thrown knives can stick in enemies or other valid targets and stay there.
3. **Return to a knife.** Press Alt to snap to the current returnable knife and reclaim it.
4. **Treat return as offense.** Knives are not only escape points—plant one next to a foe and arrive already in their face.

### Knife attacks

Qing’s knife work is more than throwing. Different attack states and items let him stab, slash, spin blades, or throw several knives at once; melee knife work is a main attack mode, not only a setup before planting returns.

### Lodged knives and return

Several returnable knives can stay in the room at once; each keeps its own point. The current target knife is reclaimed after a return; some effects can chain the next linked knife as the new return target.

A lodged knife can be an exit or the next entry. Leave one in a safe spot to escape; lodge one near enemies to close distance fast.

**The knife takes the first step; Qing arrives after.**

### Return rules

To avoid losing every return point, the system automatically keeps a necessary knife in certain cases. Ordinary throws are still limited by room geometry—this protection does not let knives ignore walls.

### Special interactions

Qing has exclusive names or lines for some items—some change knife behavior, others are only character reactions. These are special interactions, not a full attack-style compatibility map.

For example, he calls “backstab” **assassination**, comments on whether formal wear suits him, and reacts differently to his own emblem or hat. Against Disaster Heart Knife he gives a line that contrasts his assassin identity: **“Let no blood touch the blade.”**

### Tips

- **Return is not only retreat.** After a thrown knife sticks in a moving enemy, use it to close in and keep melee pressure.
- **Knife position is your next position.** When throwing, ask whether the landing is safe after return—not only whether the throw hits.
- **Do not stage every close fight.** When enemies are already on top of you, swinging or stabbing is often cleaner than throwing first.
- **Keep one knife for yourself.** Against bad terrain or bosses, a safe lodged knife gives the offense room to breathe.
