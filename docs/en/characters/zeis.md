## Mechanics

### Playstyle

> To know is already enough.

{{Character:zeis}} does not take items the moment she meets them on the road. After contact, they are sent into Death Certificate and kept as **knowledge**.

Each floor in Death Certificate, she can turn one piece of knowledge into a real permanent item—or keep it and pick a nearby item as a **reverie**, making that possibility real for this floor only.

### Knowledge and Reverie

| Choice | Duration | Result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Claim knowledge | Permanent | Permanently gain the original item and consume that knowledge |
| Choose a reverie | This floor | Temporarily gain a nearby other item; knowledge is kept |

Claiming knowledge trades future reverie options around it for a certain permanent item. Choosing a reverie temporarily forgoes that permanent gain and keeps the right to decide later.

On the next floor, reveries end and the original knowledge becomes choosable again. You may claim it permanently, pick the same reverie again, or choose another nearby reverie. A reverie never becomes new knowledge and never fans out into a second layer of reveries.

### A simple example

If item A is saved as knowledge and B, C, and D sit nearby, Zeis can claim A permanently and close future reveries from that knowledge—or take B for this floor and leave A for the next floor’s decision.

Death Certificate layouts reshuffle every floor, so next floor’s reveries around A may no longer be B, C, and D—or new options may appear.

> It is still worth thinking about.

### Actives

Actives can become knowledge too. When swapping to another active, the previous one keeps its charge and returns to its Death Certificate pedestal, still available to claim permanently or to power a reverie.

### Tips

- If the knowledge item is exactly the core piece you want long-term, you do not have to keep delaying for reveries.
- If one knowledge sits near several differently useful items, leaving it often stays more flexible than cashing it immediately; layout changes next floor may open new reveries.
- You do not need to resolve every knowledge every floor; come back when the choice looks better.
- The minimap automatically reveals Death Certificate rooms that contain knowledge you can act on. First-floor free choice, the ~150 reverie range, and temporary-pickup protections are detailed on {{System:death-certificate-knowledge}}.
