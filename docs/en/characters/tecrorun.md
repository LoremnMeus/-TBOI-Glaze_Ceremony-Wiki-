## Mechanics

### Playstyle

**Charge a reflecting light path that grows stronger on later segments, then dash through the whole route in one burst.**

While {{Character:tecrorun}} charges, a light path extends from the spear tip and reflects between room borders. More charge adds more bounce segments; **later segments are wider and hit harder.**

On release, Tecrorun dashes along the full current route at high speed. The dash is invincible and damages enemies near the path; arriving at the end also triggers a light burst.

**Charging is not only making the route longer—it is powering up the back half of the route.**

### First time with Tecrorun

1. **Start charging.** Fire direction aims the tip and shows the first light segment.
2. **Keep charging.** At each new charge tier, the path gains another bounce; later segments are wider and stronger than earlier ones.
3. **Place the strongest segments.** Prefer routing the back half through high-HP targets, not only landing the first segment.
4. **Release.** Tecrorun dashes the full route and bursts at the end.

Middle mouse toggles mouse aiming. Finishing a dash toward a door can leave the room directly.

### Charge and bounces

Bounce count comes from how far this charge went, not from how many times the path happens to hit a wall. Each new charge stage adds the next reflected segment; new later segments have higher damage and wider hitboxes, while earlier segments already drawn do not scale up with them.

### The path is the attack

The dash is not only travel along a preview: Tecrorun’s real attack volume is the whole previewed route. Early segments are good for quick hits; fully charged later segments cover more and hurt more. Against bosses, plan reflections so the powered segments pass through the target again.

### End burst

After the full dash, Tecrorun releases a light burst at the final position. More bounced segments mean higher burst damage and size; it finishes the line, but most value still comes from the powered path itself.

### Presentation

Later preview segments look brighter and wider. Dashing through different segments leaves a continuously shifting rainbow trail, and the end burst matches this route’s colors.

### Tips

- **Do not only land the first segment.** Later segments are stronger—put them on high-HP targets.
- **Longer is not always better.** What matters is placing powered segments where damage is needed.
- **The end is part of the route.** Account for the burst and where you stand after the dash.
- **Charge takes time.** Fast enemies may leave the planned path before it finishes—release early when needed.

### Trivia

Tecrorun’s mirror-reflecting light attack was inspired early on by a boss fight in *Bloodstained: Ritual of the Night*; the character’s later mechanics and identity developed independently.
