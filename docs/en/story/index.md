# Story

This section documents the formal story of *Glaze Ceremony: Promised Land*.

The public story scope of `alpha-0.1` currently ends with the **Prologue**.

Chapter I, **“What We Seek,”** is still in development and is not part of the normal playable story in this Alpha build.

To avoid exposing unfinished content, Chapter I story nodes, bosses, and related events should not be fully published on the current public Wiki.

## Prologue

The Prologue follows Qing's actions and establishes the events that lead into the later story.

This index does not currently provide a full spoiler summary. Detailed Prologue pages should only be published after their player-facing content has been reviewed.
