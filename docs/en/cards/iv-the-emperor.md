## Effects

When used, creates as many special doors as possible along the walls of the current room.

These doors can lead to other rooms already generated on the current floor, and some may lead to {{System:special-destinations|special destinations}}.

While the effect is active, the room's normal doors are temporarily closed. The generated special doors disappear after leaving the room.

## Destinations

Most doors lead to rooms that already exist on the current floor.

Some door positions can instead lead to additional special destinations. See {{System:special-destinations|Special Destinations}} for their shared rules and weights.

## Synergies

### {{Collectible:451}}

Increases the chance for usable wall positions to generate special doors, usually resulting in more doors.
