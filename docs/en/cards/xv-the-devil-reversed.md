## Effects

On use, summons Satan alongside **2–3 Devil-deal goods**. The player may buy them at normal Devil-style prices, or **hit Satan with an explosion** to abandon the deal and fight him instead.

Once the fight begins:

* All unbought goods temporarily disappear.
* The doors close and Satan enters combat.
* After Satan is defeated, every good that was still unbought before the fight reappears and can be taken **for free**.

Goods already purchased do not return. This allows the player to buy part of the offer normally, then defeat Satan to claim the remainder.

The generated goods use Devil-deal style pricing. Ordinary attacks against Satan do not start the fight.
