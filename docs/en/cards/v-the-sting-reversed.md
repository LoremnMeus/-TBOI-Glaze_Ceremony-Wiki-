## Effects

Marks the strongest enemy as a sacrifice. When it dies, the ritual picks a new victim that takes even more damage, until none remain.

Bosses and high-HP foes are preferred.

## Special interactions

### {Collectible:451}

Two sacrifices can be marked; either death advances the ritual.
