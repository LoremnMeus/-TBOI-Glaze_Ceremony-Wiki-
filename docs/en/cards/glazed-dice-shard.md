## Effects

After use, rerolls collectibles in the room and converts common basic pickups and enemies into glazed forms at the same time.

Pedestal items pick a new random result from the same color pool as their current appearance.

## Items

Each item is classified into one or more color groups based on its sprite appearance, then rerolled into another item from those groups.

An item can belong to multiple colors, so candidates may come from more than one palette.

If no other valid same-color candidate exists, that item stays unchanged.

## Glazed pickups

- {{Coin}} → {{Pickup:glaze-coin}}
- {{Heart}} → {{Pickup:glaze-heart}} / {{Pickup:glaze-heart-half}}
- {{Key}} → {{Pickup:glaze-key}}
- {{Bomb}} → {{Pickup:glaze-bomb}}
- {{GrabBag}} → {{Pickup:glaze-grabbag}}
- {{Battery}} → {{Pickup:glaze-battery}}
- {{Poop}} → {{Pickup:glaze-big-poop}}
- Chests → {{Pickup:glaze-chest}}
- Vanilla Dice Shard cards → {{Card:glazed-dice-shard}}

{{Heart}} pickups have a **70%** chance to become {{Pickup:glaze-heart-half}} and a **30%** chance to become {{Pickup:glaze-heart}}.

## Enemies

Vulnerable active enemies in the room are glazed.

Friendly enemies and some special enemies are skipped.

## Tips

When pedestal colors in the room are obvious, treat this card as a color-locked reroll: outcomes stay random, but they usually stay in a similar visual palette.
