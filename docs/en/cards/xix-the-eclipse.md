## Effects

On use, creates an eclipse vortex at Isaac's position for **15 seconds**.

Enemies near the vortex are swallowed, briefly disappear, then fall back down from above. They take **15 damage** when they land.

The vortex continues to capture enemies within its range, so enemies that land near it can be swallowed again.

The vortex remains where it was created, does not follow Isaac, and disappears when its duration ends or when the room is left.
