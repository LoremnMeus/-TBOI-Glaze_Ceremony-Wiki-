## Effects

XIV - Art? is itself a hazardous explosive. After about **4.5 seconds** on the ground, the card starts changing color and ticking as a warning; at about **6 seconds** it explodes, then bounces to another spot and restarts the timer. This cycle keeps repeating for as long as the card remains on the ground.

On use, many entity **disappearances** in the current room also trigger explosions: enemy deaths, tears or hostile projectiles vanishing, bombs being removed, pickups or familiars disappearing, and similar cases all create an explosion at that spot.

These explosions deal **2×** the player's current {{Damage}}. Pure visual effects do not trigger them, so the explosions themselves do not chain into new explosions.

The room effect from using the card lasts only for the current room and ends when leaving.

## Notes

* How an entity disappears does not matter; as long as it is a triggerable type, normal death, vanishing on hit, or removal by other effects can all cause an explosion.
* If the disappearance happens too close to any player, no explosion is created.
* Normally, explosions caused after using the card can hurt the player.
* The card's own periodic ground explosions are an inherent property of the pickup and do not require using it first.

## Synergies
