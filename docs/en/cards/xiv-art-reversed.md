## Effects

After use, when enemies, player tears, hostile projectiles, bombs, ordinary pickups, familiars, and similar entities vanish in this room, an explosion triggers at their spot. Pure visual effects do not trigger.

## Explosions

Explosion damage is about **2×** the player's current damage.

Every blast uses the same scale. Entities that vanish too close to the player do not trigger an explosion. Leaving the room ends the effect.
