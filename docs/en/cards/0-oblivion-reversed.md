## Effects

On use, temporarily gain 1 random passive item every 1 second, 50 times. Each lasts 5 seconds and then disappears.

Temporary items only provide while-held effects. They do not trigger pickup or one-time on-acquire effects, and they do not enter normal acquisition history. An item that is still active will not appear again.

Quest items, story items, and items that cannot be safely simulated as temporary effects are excluded.
