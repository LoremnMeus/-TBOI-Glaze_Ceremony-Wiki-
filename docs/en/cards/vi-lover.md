## Effects

Ordinary resources and pickups may duplicate; high-value trades and item pedestals do not.

Picking up an ordinary pickup has a **50%** chance to spawn an identical copy that cannot chain this effect.

## Mechanics

Can duplicate: coins, keys, bombs, hearts, batteries, cards/pills, normal chests.

Cannot duplicate: pedestals, shop buys, Devil deals, quest items, or Options-breaking specials.
