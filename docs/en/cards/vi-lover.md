## Effects

After use, while you remain **in this room**, picking up qualifying basic resources or **contacting** an allowed chest has a **50%** chance to create a copy.

Copies cannot trigger duplication again. Pocket pickups that can be carried and dropped back onto the floor do not trigger this effect. Leaving the room ends it.

## What can duplicate

**Can duplicate:**

- {{Heart}} hearts;
- {{Coin}} coins;
- {{Key}} keys;
- {{Bomb}} bombs;
- {{Battery}} batteries;
- {{GrabBag}} grab bags;
- {{PoopPickup}} poop pickups;
- Allowed chests (including locked and red chests); contact alone rolls duplication, even if the chest does not open on that collision.

**Cannot duplicate:**

- {{Card}} cards, runes, and soul stones;
- {{Pill}} pills;
- {{Trinket}} trinkets;
- Collectible pedestals;
- {{Shop}} goods and other priced pickups;
- Pickups already in an exclusive-choice group;
- Copies spawned by Lover itself.

## Synergies

### {{Collectible:451}}

Duplication chance rises from **50%** to **75%**.
