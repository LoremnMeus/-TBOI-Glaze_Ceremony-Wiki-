## Effects

On use, each eligible item pedestal in the room reveals several nearby alternatives around itself, with the original item and all revealed alternatives forming a single choice.

The revealed items are arranged into a small display resembling part of Zeis's Death Certificate space, recreating the way a piece of Knowledge reveals nearby Reveries.

- Alternatives are selected by moving through valid items before and after the original item, rather than by rolling from the current item pool.
- They are placed around the original pedestal where space allows; cramped areas may produce fewer alternatives.
- The original item and all revealed alternatives belong to the same choice group. Taking any one of them invalidates the rest.
- Alternatives created from shop or Devil Deal pedestals still require the appropriate payment.
- If a revealed alternative is transformed into another item before a choice is made, that alternative disappears and displays “Too greedy to take it!”.
- These revealed alternatives are not actual Reveries. Taking one grants the normal item and does not use Zeis's floor-limited Reverie rules.
