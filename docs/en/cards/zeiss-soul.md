## Effects

After use, item pedestals in the room gain extra candidates with nearby IDs, grouped as exclusive choices.

## Rules

- Needs enough space to spawn; it will not force crowded placements.
- Candidates fan out around the original item's neighboring ID range.
- After you normally take one, the other candidates disappear.
