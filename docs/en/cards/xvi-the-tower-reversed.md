## Effects

On use, **10 waves** of random obstacles begin falling into the room. Later waves generate more obstacles, causing the rain to become rapidly denser.

The falling objects are chosen from several terrain types, including different rocks, poops, locks, and TNT. Special rock variants may also appear.

Each obstacle has a **50%** chance to target a current enemy as it falls; otherwise, it drops toward a random position in the room.

## Notes

* Later waves contain more obstacles and also fall faster.
* The generated obstacles remain in the room afterward as actual terrain.
* Once started, the waves continue on their fixed timing and do not stop early when the room is cleared.
* Generated obstacles retain their own terrain type, so special rocks, TNT, and similar objects keep their usual properties.

## Synergies

### {{Collectible:451}}

The number of waves increases from **10** to **16**. Because later waves are already denser, the six additional waves also generate substantially more obstacles.
