## Effects

When used, replays every other card previously used in the current room.

The Sun only records cards actually used in this room. Cards triggered indirectly by other effects are not recorded.

After The Sun is used, the cards it replayed are cleared from its history. New cards must be used before they can be replayed again.

If a single use of The Sun replays at least **3 cards**, it also spawns **1 random card**.

## Synergies

### {{Collectible:451}}

If a single use replays at least **10 cards**, it also spawns **1 collectible**.
