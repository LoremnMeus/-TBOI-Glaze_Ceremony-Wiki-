## Effects

On use, enemies already present in the current room randomly demand one resource:

- {{Coin}} 1 coin;
- {{Key}} 1 key;
- {{Bomb}} 1 bomb;
- {{Heart}} 1 red heart.

The requested resource is displayed above the enemy.

If you have the requested resource, stop firing and approach the enemy to automatically lift it above your head. Touch the enemy to give it and satisfy its demand.

Starting to fire or moving away immediately puts the resource away without spending it.

## Satisfying a Demand

Satisfied enemies remain hostile. They continue moving, firing, and using their normal attacks.

However, their bodies can no longer deal contact damage to players.

Satisfied normal enemies also stop blocking room clear. They may remain alive and continue attacking even after the room has opened.

Satisfied bosses still need to be defeated to clear the room.

When a satisfied enemy dies:

- normal enemies return **1** of the requested resource, with a **35%** chance to return 1 additional copy;
- bosses always return **2**.

You can therefore pay a normal enemy and leave it alive, or continue fighting it to recover the resource you paid with a chance to gain one extra.

Lure only marks enemies that already exist when it is used. Newly spawned, summoned, or later-wave enemies do not receive a demand. Using another Lure later can mark currently unmarked enemies, but it will not reroll or change the Tarot Cloth state of enemies that already have a demand.

## Synergies

### {{Collectible:451}}
