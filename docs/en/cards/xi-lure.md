## Effects

When used, enemies in the current room randomly demand **1 coin, 1 key, 1 bomb, or 1 red heart**. The demand is shown above the enemy.

If you have the matching resource, stop firing and approach the enemy to give it to them.

Satisfied normal enemies keep attacking, but **no longer deal contact damage and no longer block room clear**. Satisfied bosses must still be defeated normally.

Killing a satisfied enemy drops the resource it demanded:

* Normal enemies drop **1**, with a **35%** chance to drop 1 extra;
* Bosses always drop **2**.

## Rules

Lure only affects enemies that already exist when it is used; enemies that appear later do not automatically gain a demand.
