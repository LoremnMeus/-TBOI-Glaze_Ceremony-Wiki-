## Effects

After use, the effect lasts for **30 seconds**. Normal attacks are disabled during this time; attacking summons Mom's giant foot instead.

Mom's foot deals **40x the player's current damage**, and the interval between attacks scales with the player's fire rate.

The effect persists between rooms. Using the card again adds to the remaining duration.

## Synergies

### {{Collectible:451}}

Increases the duration from **30 seconds** to **60 seconds**.
