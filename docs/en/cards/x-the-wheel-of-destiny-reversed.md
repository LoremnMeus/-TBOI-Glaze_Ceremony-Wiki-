## Effects

After use, each qualifying ordinary pickup in the current room expands into a group of **3–5** mutually exclusive options that spin around its original position.

Touch one option to take it; the remaining options in that group disappear.

Using the card again adds **3–5** more options to each existing spinning group. No matter how many options a group already has, each group is expanded only once per use.

## Convertible pickups

The following pickups can be converted:

- {{Heart}} Hearts
- {{Coin}} Coins
- {{Key}} Keys
- {{Bomb}} Bombs
- {{Poop}} Poop pickups
- {{GrabBag}} Sacks
- {{Pill}} Pills
- {{Battery}} Batteries
- {{Card}} Cards and runes
- {{Trinket}} Trinkets

Collectible pedestals are not affected.

Pickups that already belong to another choice group are not forcibly absorbed into a Wheel group.

Eligible priced pickups retain their original price.

## Quantity limits

When the room already contains many eligible pickups and Wheel options, the number of new options added per group is reduced:

- Below **64**: normal amount;
- **64–69**: at most 4 new options per group;
- **70–74**: at most 3;
- **75–79**: at most 2;
- At **80**, the card stops generating additional options.

Existing options are never removed by these limits.
