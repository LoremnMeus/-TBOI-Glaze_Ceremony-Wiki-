## Effects

When used, removes all {{Coin}} coins, {{Key}} keys and {{Bomb}} bombs currently held and converts them into permanent stats for the current run.

Each resource has a different conversion value:

- {{Bomb}} Bombs × 1
- {{Key}} Keys × 0.8
- {{Coin}} Coins × 0.4

The conversion increases damage, tears, range, speed and luck.

More resources provide a stronger boost, but further conversions gradually give diminishing returns.

Only resources held at the moment the card is used are converted. Coins, keys and bombs obtained afterward are unaffected.

## Synergies

### {{Collectible:451}}

The full amount of resources still counts toward the stat conversion, then about half of the converted coins, keys and bombs are returned.
