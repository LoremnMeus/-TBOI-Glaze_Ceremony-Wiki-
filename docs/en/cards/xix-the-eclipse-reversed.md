## Effects

On use, begins a **3-minute** eclipse.

As the sun gradually draws closer, two kinds of falling light repeatedly strike the room:

* Red strikes target enemies and deal **3× the player's current {{Damage}}**.
* Orange strikes target the player's position and must be avoided.

Both kinds of strikes become increasingly frequent as the eclipse continues, causing the effect to grow steadily more dangerous over time.

The eclipse persists between rooms and ends when the 3 minutes expire or upon entering a new floor.

## Synergies

### {{Collectible:451}}

Enemy-targeting strikes increase from **3×** to **6× the player's current {{Damage}}**.

Player-targeting orange strikes remain unchanged, as do the duration and escalation rate.
