## Effects

After use, **reversed Tarot cards** that appear later on the current floor are converted into their corresponding upright versions.

The effect lasts until leaving the current floor.

## Conflict

If {{Card:xii-the-hanged-man}} and {{Card:xii-the-hanged-man-reversed}} are active at the same time, the next Tarot card affected by their conversion effects enters a conflict between its upright and reversed forms.

After the conflict, both conversion effects end and the player is sent to the {{ErrorRoom}}.
