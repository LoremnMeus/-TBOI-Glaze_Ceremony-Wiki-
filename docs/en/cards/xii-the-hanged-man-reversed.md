## Effects

After use, **reversed Tarot cards** that appear later on the current floor are converted into their corresponding upright versions.

The effect lasts until leaving the current floor.

## Conflict

If {{Card:xii-the-hanged-man}} and {{Card:xii-the-hanged-man-reversed}} are both active, a Tarot card affected by both conversions causes a conflict and sends the player to the {{ErrorRoom}}.

Both conversion effects end after the conflict.
