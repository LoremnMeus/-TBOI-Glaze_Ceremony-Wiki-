## Effects

On the ground, this soul stone flies toward enemies and explodes on its own; active use triggers a safe explosion at the player.

## Rules

- Explosion damage is about **50**, with a large radius, and by default does not hurt the player.
- The chase phase also applies lightning-style collision damage.
