## Effects

On use, creates a **50-damage explosion** at the player's position. This explosion cannot damage players.

While Anna's Soul remains on the ground, it repeatedly attacks enemies on its own:

- After a short delay, it enters a black hole and temporarily disappears from view.
- While hidden, it targets a nearby enemy.
- It then reappears above the target and crashes down.
- On landing, it creates a **50-damage explosion**.
- After the attack, it becomes collectible again. If left on the ground, it repeats the cycle.
