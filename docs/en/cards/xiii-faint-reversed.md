## Effects

After use, the current room gradually falls asleep.

As long as you stop firing, sleep depth keeps rising; **moving does not wake the room**.

As sleep deepens, enemies, hostile projectiles, and hostile lasers grow slower.

At deepest sleep, enemies are reduced to **10%** speed, while hostile projectiles and hostile lasers are reduced to **5%**; they are never completely frozen.

Firing again gradually wakes the room; leaving the room ends the effect.

## Synergies

### {{Collectible:451}}

The room falls asleep faster, and wakes more slowly after you start firing again.
