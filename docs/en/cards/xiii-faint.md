## Effects

When used, spawns a bed that can be slept in.

Sleeping restores health normally. After waking, the player is teleported to **a random room on the current floor**.

After waking, rooms on the current floor become strange and dim, as though the player had arrived somewhere unfamiliar.

Sometimes, a **broken bed** appears instead. It can still be slept in and restores health normally, and the same change to the floor still occurs, but it **does not teleport the player**.
