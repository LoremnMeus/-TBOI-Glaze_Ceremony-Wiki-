## Effects

On use, teleports Isaac to a {{SuperSecretRoom}} on the current floor. If multiple Super Secret Rooms exist, one is chosen at random.

If no usable Super Secret Room exists on the floor, the card performs a normal teleport instead.

If this card is held into the next floor, **1 additional {{SuperSecretRoom}}** is generated on that floor.

## Synergies

### {{Collectible:451}}

Holding the card into the next floor generates **2 additional {{SuperSecretRoom}}s** instead of 1.
