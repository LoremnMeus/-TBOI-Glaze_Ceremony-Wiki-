## Effects

When used, choose **1 item you currently hold**. That item is temporarily removed and turned into **2 fragile matching item wisps**.

Each wisp is destroyed after taking a single hit. After passing through **2 new floors**, each surviving wisp turns back into **1 copy of the original item**.

If both wisps survive, you can therefore receive **2 copies of the original item**. Wisps that disappear early do not return an item.

The selection can be cancelled, in which case the card is returned.

## Rules

* Multiple sets of unresolved item wisps can exist at the same time.
* Progress is made when entering a new floor; changing rooms does not advance the timer.
* Each wisp survives or expires independently.
