## Effects

On use, enemies in the current room are feared and take about **3× their normal total damage**.

Enemies that appear later in the same room receive the same effect automatically.

As a drawback, the player is feared for **20 seconds**.

## Notes

* Fear already applied to enemies can last for about **2 minutes**.
* Leaving the room stops the effect from being applied to enemies that appear afterward.
* The roughly **3× total damage** comes from additional damage dealt on top of the original hit; it does not triple the player's {{Damage}} stat.

## Synergies

### {{Collectible:451}}

The player no longer suffers the **20-second fear**. Enemy fear and the roughly 3× total-damage effect are unchanged.
