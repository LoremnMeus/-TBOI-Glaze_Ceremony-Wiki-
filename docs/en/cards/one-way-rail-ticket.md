## Effects

On use, the player holds up the One-Way Rail Ticket. Pressing a fire direction then calls in a train from outside the room, sending it straight across the room.

- Trains travel only in the four cardinal directions.
- The train follows the same damage, obstacle-breaking, player-collision, and train-collision rules as the train summoned by the Round-Trip Rail Ticket.
- Using the One-Way Rail Ticket does not create another ticket.
- The One-Way Rail Ticket does not normally appear in the card pool; it is generated by the Round-Trip Rail Ticket.
