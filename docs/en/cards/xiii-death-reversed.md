## Effects

Drops actives, cards/pills, and trinkets. While those slots stay empty, heavily boosts Damage, Tears, Range, Speed, and Luck. Secondary/pocket actives return to their slots when picked up.

Any required-empty slot being filled again ends all boosts.

## Stats (reference)

Normal: Damage +2.5, Tears +1.5, Range +3, Speed +0.5, Luck +5.

Tarot Cloth: Damage +3.5, Tears +2, Range +4, Speed +0.7, Luck +7.
